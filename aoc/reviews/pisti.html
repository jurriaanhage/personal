The music

This album is a bit groovy, a bit spacy, a bit poppy, even a bit of surfpunk, sort of a lot of stuff put together. It's not really progressive, but with its odd meters and oddball instrument use there's some attraction for those into music out of the ordinary. The clear trumpet reminds of the early Chicago brass bands Chicago and Blood, Sweat & Tears, but also of the more or less current band Cake. The Hungarian vocals make very clear, though, this band originates from a different part of the world. I didn't like this mini much at first, but on repeated listens it does reveal a certain charm. Tentatively positive on this one. The disc contains MP3 versions of the band's earlier CDs, giving it the bulk at which the regular playing part fails.
