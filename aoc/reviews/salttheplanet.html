Summary

Jeremy has made his share of records, but this is my first encounter with his music.

The music

Jungle is quite a modern opener: house rhythms, a bit of a worldlike atmosphere, but not very relaxed. The soft tapestries weaved by the keyboards belie the relative neuroticism of the track. Reversed percussion have their place in the serene Waterfall. In addition to the tapestries there are also some tinkling keyboards. The melodic part is rather quiet, but again the percussion is a little too pronounced. I guess it comes from being modern. Later on the percussion drops out. Whirlwind is something different. At first it seems almost industrial, but the soothing keyobards are still there. The percussion here is very mechanic and I'm not fond of it. In a sense I'm not really satisfied with the two facedness of the music. The mechanical percussion and the soothing layers of keyboards beneath. I have really no idea why this track should be called Whirlwind, since there's little whirling going on. Heartbeat continues on the same footing. Strangely enough I do not hear a heartbeat here, but a sonar. On the title track, the keyboards are a little more pronounced. Melodically the track is okay. In the middle the music almost comes to a standstill, but then it picks up again giving me an impression of deserts and camels. Earthquake is quite a heavy track which even reminded me of King Crimson at times. A plodding, rather violent piece, and the best one up to now. The closer is Lightyears. A very melodic piece with some nice electric guitar both in the middle as well as at the end. The music is maybe a little too accessible and friendly.

Conclusion

On the whole not a very satisfying album. This has much to do with the two-sidedness of the music. The modern percussion on one hand and the very warm and soothing keyboards on the other. Although I can admit liking the synth part, in retrospect I cannot admit having heard many melodies that stuck. Often I really don't see what the titles have to do with the music and like Mr. Fripp told that has everything to do with it. Earthquake is quite a good track and although it is easy to listen to this kind of music, it leaves little impression.
