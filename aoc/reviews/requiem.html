Summary

Having had met Bernd Blecha through e-mail, I was told he'd sent some material, but it never materialized. When I went to Zwolle to interview Asgard, some guys with the band gave me this CD and it had Fallen Foe in the title. Now, Bernds band was called Fallen Foe, so I thought....and yes on the backside was his e-mail address. It also gave those guys the idea that I know what I'm talking about. Thank God, I know better than that. This is their own product, but they migh start a label with Asgard in the future.

The music

The first track Requiem is a nice, but typical intro for a progressive rock album.

In Black we find already that the vocals aren't very good and also that the album is lacking in the production. The songs sound more like unfinished demo tracks than anything else. The melodies are sometimes nice and the lyrics (although not always very rhythmic) have a meaning for every song, all thoroughly explained in the booklet, but the drums sound flat, the vocals are weak, the lyrics fit badly to the music and the keyboards are well in the back. There are some offbeats rhythms and also it has its quotum of signature changes, but the neo-prog compositions sound standard and cliche although for instance Ich moecht.. is not only in German but it is funky/groovy as well and introduces the saxophone into their music. The Little Whale has really some nice melodies with a good intro and some nice guitar, but the lyrics are not very good, although the subject might be. In the nice parts it sounds a lot like Twelfth Night, but without the energy. I said: Musik is acoustic with good vocal melodies. The singer sounds rather naked here, but I like it. Not progressive, but it sounds more meaningful than anything I've heard up til now. Some parts of Exculpation from Sorcery are also nice, but even then I wouldn't call it a good track. Unerfuellte Liebe is again more a popsong than prog. Casual Ward sounds rather familiar, but because the vocal melody is so very recognizable, it might be from some of my previous listens. Here it is shown that harmonies are not there strongest point, but the lyrics on the downfall of a succesful man are nice. The longest track Crusade has a nice guitarsolo in the middle, but the rest of the track is not very good and again I'm not satisfied.

The added live part of a ghosttrack doesn't really help either.

Conclusion

Sorry to say that this record does not fit my standards and you can read up there why. Amateuristic and demo-like are the terms that best fit here. This is a band that is still looking for something and now that the guiarist has left (the one that seemed to have something going for him) I'm afraid it is better that like the singer said, the band will be better at home making music in the line of Herbert Groenemeyer.
