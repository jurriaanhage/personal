Quikion - Hallelujah!!

Artist: Quikion
Title: Hallelujah!!
Label: Poseidon Records PRM-001
Length(s): 32 minutes
Year(s) of release: 1997
Month of review: [12/2003]

Line up

Totoki Yukiko - vocal, concertina, rhythm machine
Sasaki Emi - accordeon, glockenspiel
Oguma Eiji - acoustic guitar

Tracks

1) Hallelujah!! 5.28
2) Voyage (translated from Japanese) 4.41
3) The Music Box (translated from Japanese) 6.33
4) Train Song 4.08
5) Summer Time (translated from Japanese) 5.08
6) TV 5.41

Summary

This album contains early material of Quikion. Tracks 2, 3 and 5 have Japanese titles only.

The music

Opener Hallelujah!! brings an almost French feel, with friendly acoustic accompaniement, including guitar and accordeon. The vocals are in Japanese, but not in the semi hysterical way we sometimes hear, they are musical and sweet. The track is pretty typical for the album. The accordeon is very dominant, making it easy to imagine hearing these sounds while taking a walk at Montmartre. If it weren't for the Japanese vocals, that is. In some tracks the vocals are just a bit more dominant, deminishing the Montmarte feel right away. Closer TV, due to the howling wind in the back, has more a wintery feel.

Conclusion

Hallelujah!! is a rather odd album, but it has definite charm. I wouldn't call it progressive though. If you like a bit of light accordeon chanson, you will enjoy this, though, with such sweet songs.
