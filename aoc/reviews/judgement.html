Summary

These guys went from epic grind to melodic alternative rock and that is where they are still at. Their Alternative 4 was a great record with great songs and great melodies. I heard some good things about Judgement which is why I'll review it here.

The music

Deep is build on a acoustic guitar melody, low key singing with various good vocal melodies. The moves right into the powerful Pitiless. The loud rhythm guitars are really pressing here. Again, a perfect song, noisy and driving. After a quiet intermezzo the guitar screams out, rending. Forgotten Hopes may remind some of Pink Floyd at their most depressing (Hey You!). The noisy guitar contrasts with this hopelessness with its high pitched screaming sound. Destiny Is Dead is the conclusion as it were to the first four songs, the first time the music really fades away. We start over with Make It Right. In this song the spirit of The Tea Party is somewhat alive. In fact, if you'd want a band with a similar feel then that might be the one. However, it seems that The Tea Party is a band with quite some Arabic influences, while Anathema does not have this. The dark side of Anathema also reminds me of Sad Lovers And Giants, but Anathema is warmer. Make It Right has quite some extensive atmospheric keyboard work making the song sound quite majestic. One Last Goodbye is a mor acoustic piece, again quite depressing sounding, but with a subtle vocal melody. Towards the end of the song the singing becomes quite desperate. Beautiful. Parisienne Moonlight brings us melancholic piano and female vocals. The title track is quite a varied track with in the middle the music almost derailing. In Don't Look Too Far the angelic vocals of the female vocalist (also present in Parisienne Moonlight) combine with the slightly vocoded vocals of Cavanagh and a psychedelic guitar sound. Floyd returns in the opening of Emotional Winter. The title speaks for itself I would say in this ballad like track that shows its rough edges in the chorus. Wings Of God is the longest track, just over 6 minutes. After the previous rather relaxed track, this is an energetic one with a strong surging aspect to the music, with washes of drums and effects and a screaming guitar. The album ends with the calm instrumental 2000 & Gone.

Conclusion

This is not prog, but should be likable for prog lovers. The sound of the band is really more "alternative" or "gothic" than "progressive", but I feel that everyone into rock music should at least have tried this band out, since they constitute one of the brightest lights and most underrated bands at this moment. Large words maybe, but that is simply how I (in all my wisdom) see it. With this album they show that Alternative 4 was no lucky shot and there hopefully there is more in store.
