Summary

Over 70 minutes worth of what the band calls a non-opera (in celebration of nothingness).

The music

Hmm, yes. A hard album to review that's for sure. The Descent is just a narrator introducing the story of the all-conquering zeros, bringing cold to the hearts of humans. Rolling Terror tells of their advent but here some slight semblance of music comes in. Pamela Zero has a very flexible voice ranging from low to very high and she uses it without shame. Zero Cold contains some free form jazz with lots of saxophone and the many voices of Zero. In a sense one might compare her vocalization to that of Nina Hagen. In strangeness the music is comparable to the experimental side of Frank Zappa enjoying the same irony, but in a more serious way. Daight Time is just vocals. Throughout the albums shards of punctuating percussion can be heard and as in Playfully The Zeros, sometimes a saxophone pops up and rears its ugly head. Most Of Them Already Are brings the panic to a head when the Third Victim is turned into a zero by the zeros. Multiply By Zero brings us the free jazz of earlier on, again with those estranging vocals. Lots of bass and some keyboards on this track as well. Spin starts with the vocals of Zero telling how a zero takes over a family and devours them, making them into zeros. There's also some reversed music in this track, sounding quite like space rock. One Together, Two The Same uses vocoding to add an extra dimension to the spoken words of the Fifth Victim, slightly warping what he says. In Enter Blank enters the preacher on the stage introducing to us the biggest zero of them all: Blank. With We Will Watch You Die we return to Playfully , The Zeros. The gist of this Bodysnatchers-like story seems to be that people have been turned into zeros already. In Reconfiguration the singing is relatively normal utilizing vocal melodies. The vocal melodies, though sung in discordant unison, remind me of psalm melodies. Truly has a Celtic Clannad-ring to it. Be And Be And Be And Be With Us is one of the more musical songs being almost a real song. Again space rock and free jazz reign and reign mightily, in weirdness and irony this band also comes quite close to Gong, although Gong is more musically oriented. In Wake Up Warm Up the solution is given: we should divide by zero and thus become infinity (a mathematical "truth" in fact) , becoming so warm we can melt the zeros. The singing here is almost gospel.

Conclusion

Space Rock, free jazz, weird vocals and effects travelling from ear to ear, well in fact just what they or the accompanying reviews promised. This is one weird album that might find fans among the lovers of Frank Zappa or the RIO side of progressive. The music on this album is rather sparse, the story being the focal point. The sparse music that is present is either rather fragmented and consists of shards of sounds, generally. As such I cannot judge this album for its music, since the role it plays just is not big enough. The story is quite funny in a way, mostly in the rather ironic way it has been put into words. Still, knowing the story after a few listens it is hard to judge what is left. There are no real songs on this album and I wonder if the theatre format might not be a better format for a project like this. I understand however that if you as a band want to contact as many people as possible, than theatre is not the way.
