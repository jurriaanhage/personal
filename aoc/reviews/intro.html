Summary

They have one album to their name, Alterando las Divisiones from 1989, and you might guess from the name that they are rather Rush influenced. It turns out they are somewhat heavier and more directed towards progmetal, but with enough melodic content.

The music

The album starts out really nice with a melodic track, very to the point, mid-tempo, so not that quick or heavy and with a nice melody in the chorus. More of the same in the next track, but here the vocals, being high and a little wavery and thin, start to impress themselves on the listener.

Overall the tracks are rather up-tempo and in a way they sound optimistic.

The nervousness of La Villa Strangiato is present in 451, but there's some nice melody there as well. The problem is however that you hear it only marginally. Also probably the first progmetal track to feature bagpipes.

The next track has some ELPish keys, but after the intro we arrive in the heavy mid-tempo part with the vocals a bit too thin and high for my liking (squeak if you know what I mean). Although the vocal melody isn't very special, the melody for the chorus is again very nice, but the singing kills it rather. The song has also its share of breaks and my opinion is that sometimes it tends to be a little too much.

Again an ELPish intro on this track, which is more straightforward than the previous. All in all a rather playful track with again (again?) a nice melody in the chorus.

Analogias is a driven track in which the singing is a bit whining, but this time it fits the music. This ballad of the album is a driven piece of work, and although the song isn't perfect (the singing could still be better) it is one of the better tracks on the album showing promise for the future. In a good way the end of it reminds me of the closing of Prince's Purple Rain. Notwithstanding the heavy sound a very attractive and heavy track with enough room for all instrumentalists to do their stuff.

The next one is starts with a handfull of breaks and thus a little tiring. Often with this kind of music I have the feeling that it does not really have any purpose for the song. The guitarsolo is nice though. After a quiet interlude, there's a lonely guitarsolo with sparse accompaniment.

A nice intro to the next track, although it is a little too repetitive. The vocals parts aren't very good, and at the end we get into some freestyle jamming on part of the band.

Conclusion

Being no fan of high squeaky voices, this is a hard album to enjoy very much and it took me quite a listen to determine the bands strongpoints. First of all we have the very melodic parts which I really tend to like, but as a whole the music is a little fragmented and the vocals are not only not very much to my liking, but they also sound uncontrolled. As a whole, the band still isn't cohesive enough and sound a little well, amateuristic, or more likely: they sound like they are playing live. The tracks in which the vocals annoy me least are not surprisingly the ones which I like best, being the first and sixth one. Also, some tracks contain a little too many breaks for my taste. So not very recommended, but if they polish (not Polish) up their act, they might get somewhere. They have a feeling for melody, which is rather rare in these areas (of music).
