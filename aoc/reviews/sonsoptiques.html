Summary

This debut was originally released in 1979, and rereleased with two bonus tracks. It shows somewhat experimental keyboard work.

The music

Despite Breant being a keyboardplayer, this isn't by far the kind of album you'd expect from for instance Jean Michel Jarre. Quite to the contrary: although electronics are dominant throughout the whole, the sound is pretty diverse. There are some pretty eclectic tracks as Les Journaux Annocent La Guerre, but just as well more melodic and electronic sounding stuff as Survol De Rio, with pretty much everything else in between present. The overall result is an album that's pretty balanced, definitely deserving this rerelease. The two bonus tracks were recorded in 2001 by Breant by himself and sound a bit more electronic.
