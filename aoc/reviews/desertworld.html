Summary

The music

This is the fourth installment of the seemingly spacerock oriented outfit Brainstorm. The bio sheet contains a load of blabber about travels to outer space and the band's final return to terrestrial form. The artwork didn't help much either: the five guys in an American fiftees convertible -minus the wheels- floating through space. As you might guess: I wasn't impressed with this. But as I put on the disc it became apparent that this was not just another album filled with gratuitous flotational music of the non-commital type.

Opener The Light is very much a progressive track with long instrumental sections with relatively minor space influences. Occupation is based on a groovy almost dub-type of rhythm that receives a bit more attention than it really deserves. The melodic interspersions with bluesy feel take a good step in making up for this. But still, the level reached in the opening track remains outside its reach. The tracks following this inhabit more space-type influences, and are less progressively oriented. Take Paradise Lost for example. The music is tranquil, almost sedate. The vocals peaceful, with dominant flute. Nice as this maybe, after near nine minutes the less than astute listener will be lulled to sleep. Closer Desert World is more firm, has more progressive content. It is strange to note that the two longest tracks on the disc have the strongest definition, and the least tendency to just float away.

Technically this album is far from pristine. The sound is somewhat muffled, the volume low and not constant across the album, and seems to trail off towards the end of the album.

Conclusion

Brainstorm is less flotational than they might appear at first sight. The music is a combination of progressive and spacerock, really. That doesn't mean, however, that there is not a significant amount of sections that are mostly dwiddly, meandering, or gratuitous in some other form. This is a disc that is pretty nice, has its moments, but that lacks the staying power to keep the attention while it's running, let alone after it's done. Floaters will probably enjoy this enough, progressive lovers better put their attention elsewhere.
