Summary

A German progmetal band with two known and three unknown members.

The music

Aggressivively opening with the track named after the band, this seems at first a not very interesting track. However, the heavy driving basswork, the dark guitar sound and the recognizable chorus made me change my opinion, showing there is more to this band. Sick Society has a slower tempo. The vocalist is still putting on his raunchiest vocals. The music is generally quite powerful, but enjoys a softer dark interlude that easily flows into something akin to panic, and then returns to the catchy chorus. Browsing is about www, a hobby of mine. On a powerful rhythm section this is like the first track a more thrashy one. The melody is a bit less interesting though. The title track is an instrumental one (if we discounted the breathing). A dark atmospheric track that moves right into Psycho Lover. Almost the longest song with slightly over six minutes, this is not really a great track. Some rest is there in Awakening. There's some heaviness here, but also melody. Power and speed returns in Rapacious Attack (not really surprising). This is really much faster than one usually encounters on progmetal albums. Desire shows us a significantly different face: an emotive track with a guitar solo no less (after all the heavy guitarwork). Really, this is quite a nice track, a bit too "sweet" perhaps, but it works and that's what counts. Different I's is a slow, grinding song built on a low grumbling bass, grating vocals, and powerful chords. Escape From Reality is with slightly under seven minutes the longest track on the album. Not that it has epic qualities, but the track is okay in my book. The closer is the fast Thrill Of It All, more plain metal than prog metal I would say.

Conclusion

More for a metal fan, than a prog fan, that is clear. The music is not bad, but it lacks the epic qualities that I feel "real progmetal" should have to be interesting. If you do not mind trashy tracks and rough, but strong, vocals than this might be one to check out. The band does offer a lot of variation between tracks and although they might pay a little more attention to vocal melodies and such, the powerful driving way in which they packaged their music has something to say for it. I especially liked the strong rhythm section in this band.
