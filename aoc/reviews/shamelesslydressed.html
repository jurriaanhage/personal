Summary

Got this CD single from a friend of mine to say something about it. I have had no contact with the people of the band, so

The music

The music on this single is quite close to what would ordinarily have been released on the SI Music label (similar to Timelock, PTS, ...). The first song is built around a great melody played repetitively on piano. The lead vocalist has a pleasant, somewhat emotional voice, but the vocal harmonies could have been a bit better. I'm reminded of the later Styx, especially because of some of the vocal parts. In Different Silence is more up-beat and again the melody is a good one (somewhat similar to the one of the previous song). This song is more rock based and contains a few breaks making it sound more progressive. I'm still not that fond of the harmonies. Empty Hands is with nine minutes the longest track. Opening with keyboards and the blowing of the wind this promises to become a more epic piece. Long powerful chords on guitar and some forceful drumming, but the vocal melody leads the way. Again the harmonies are quite prominent. In atmosphere this song is quite different from the previous ones, but it is recognizably the same band.

Conclusion

Accessible, catchy and melodic, but not lightheaded if you know what I mean. The band still has some maturing to do, but already they have a sound of their own and show a lot of promise.
