Summary

The music

This album by Thunder Storm is pretty heavy all around, but keeping ample room for melodic sections. That combination creates a rather listenable heavy album. Where normally tracks on such as albums as this one tend to lose themselves in endless sections of heavyness, Thunder Storm manage to incorporate sudden twists in a number of them (such as Parallel Universe), that are just that bit of the unexpected to keep the attention. Having said that, the majority of tracks is a little more regular, such as Unchanging Words and Electric Funeral. I have to say though, that the vocal harmonies sometimes remind me of Vanden Plas.

Conclusion

Quite a nice album in its vein, but more for those interested in heavy rock of the Deep Purple kind, than for fans of progressive music.
