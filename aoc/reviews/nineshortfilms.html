Summary

The music

From the start of the album we are confronted with an atmosphere that seems to be pressing in. Sheehan's bass is oppressive, Bozzio's whispered feverish vocals have an urgency about them which appears nervous. The guitar synths sound eerie, completed by the ever changing percussive instruments, with dito rhythms. Only towards the end longer instrumental sections seem to lift the cloak. Which I rather liked, really.

Conclusion

This album is as much about its atmosphere as it is about its songs, forming a whole which is rather fascinating in its closeness. Good stuff.
