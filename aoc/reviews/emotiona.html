The music

I was rather surprised with this release, because I had expected something entirely different. More progressive to be honest. What we have here is a band that plays songs like F.A.F. do. This means that the tracks are accessible and melodic and there a lot of talk of love and that kind of thing. Now, there's nothing wrong with love and to be honest, the music world would be off far worse if it weren't for love (or rather its problems).

Still, most of the tracks make it compositionally, although at times it's very accessible. It does contain some parts that you will never find on a run-of-the-mill melodic product, so it's not that obvious.

No, really little too complain with this album, although I was afraid that I would have a lot too at first. This albums contains mostly low-key and low or mid-tempo tracks with a some jazz influences in 7 (also some De Lucia there) and track 8, being the bummer of the album, a rocker with little content.

The rest of tracks are well-crafted and some of them have at least a little variety in them and some even quite a lot like the surprisingly bouncy Suddenly. The best track on the album turns out to be At the End of the Bridge which is a captivating ballad (or lament).

Conclusion

Not very progressive, but more in the FAF style with good melodies and a bad rocker (well, it's short). The vocals are sympathetic and carry emotion, although at first they may sound a bit flat and there's just that tinge of progressiveness to keep it interesting, but don't expect too much on that account. With some back up a track like At the End of the Bridge might have been a hit somewhere.
