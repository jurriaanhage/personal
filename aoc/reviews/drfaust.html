Summary

With two albums to their name their are only two people left in the band Aton's. I do have Caccia Grossa which is rather average. I was hoping for some progression. Let's see what happened.

The music

The first track is partly spoken. A dialoque between God and Mephistofeles. The second part of track is music alright and the first thing that struck me was the bad sound. It seems like this album was underproduced or something. The vocals sound far off and the drums are too much up front and rather irritating, also because the other instruments like the keys and the guitar are also way back in the mix. The melodies might be alright, but if you can't hear them well there's little that can be done. Still, once in a while the guitar sound becomes heavier and then this problem is compensated for. Still that's hardly an excuse.

The album contains mostly quiet music with like I said an occaissonal heavy guitar and also, mostly in track 4 some folky and jazzy influences. Track 5 has some interesting signatures, but for the most of it this album contains not overly aggressive or fired up melodic "rock" with vocalizations like the ones by Everon although less well produced and with less power.

Conclusion

Not a very interesting release. They weren't really known for their powerful music but it seems that with the leaving of a number of members, that the fire has gone out entirely. Also for a third album, I really think the production/mix should have been better.
