The music

Despite being founded in 1997, Dialogue With Mirror And God is Lost In Tears' debut. It turned out to be a heavy gothic album, not unlike the older Paradise Lost material. And I'd have to say that it's a pretty okay album in its genre, the problem is, though, that the tracks don't set themselves apart from eachother all that much. This creates on the one hand a highly consistent album, but on the other a somewhat blend one, of which you can pick any number of tracks to get a good idea of what it's about. Well, except maybe for the opener Intro and the closing ballad So Cold, which due to their own character easily draw the attention. In conclusion a pretty good album, but maybe a bit more consistent than would be good.
