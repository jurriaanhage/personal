Summary

You shall have to forgive about the track titles, because the booklet I got was not specific about all of them and I did some guesswork.

The music

We open with a typical orchestral overture with male ahhhs and strings. Spacepenetrator shows the real face of this Finnish band which combines the use of lots of synths with double bass drums, death vocals (somewhat understandable I have to admit). The music is fast and is comparable to say The Haunting by Sear Bliss. The music seems complex and full, but when you listen closely, you hear that the composition and melodies are rather straightforward and much is hidden beneath the layer of noise that the band makes. The music hence is really very tuneful and almost waltz like.

From Behind The Black Rainbow is really very much the same with hasty drumming laced with orchestral synths. The vocals are biting. On Under The Cloak Of Darkness the fast paced orchestral parts are alternated with more industrial outings. The same holds for Ardor For Euphoria, but the song and melodies are a bit stronger here.

With 6th Day the similarities get to be a bit too much. I would hope the band would spend more time on variety between their songs. Aha, but this takes place on Channel 35, a beautiful acoustic intermezzo. An Abstract Of Inertia however takes us back to the hasty orchestral progmetal, which however does not come near to such other contenders as Rhapsody for instance. The title track does include a break into something different.

Conclusion

For such a short album remarkably little variation on this hasty tuneful and orchestral variant on Sear Bliss. The vocals I could stand, but be warned: they can be grating.
