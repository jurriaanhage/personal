The music

Elegy is put forward as one of the most solid acts in the prog metal scene, but their music is more a blend of an Yngwie Malmsteen based metal and a Magnum based pomp rock. Originally a Dutch band (which they still claim to be), now, after Patrick Rondat and Ian Parry joining, more an international act, both in membership and musical direction, leaning much more to an American market. Due to the blend of styles the album remains safe, albeit by only a small margin, from the threatening monotony of pomp rock and the ego tripping of Malmsteen style, making it quite listenable. If this were your cup of tea (it isn't mine, in case you were still wondering), you would probably consider this a pretty good, even great album. To me it's merely ok.
