Summary

Starting off as a magazine, SI Music became the record company associated with the magazine. On this album we find twelve tracks coming from twelve of the first twenty releases of the label.

The music

On this low budget sampler most of the tracks indicate fairly well the contents and quality of the albums they represent. Because of its cheapness, a good initiation into the label.
