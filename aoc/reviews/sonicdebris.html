Summary

Hailing from Norway this band was formerly known under the name Blindfold. Having send their demo CD to SI Music, it finally ended up in my mailbox and I decided to give a short review of this disc right here.

The music

The music on this disc is quite loud and raw, but especially the choruses are full of good melody. The style is mostly reminiscent of more modern bands such as Radiohead, but a bit louder and rawer. The band does not avoid experimentation such as in the second track Virtual Steps, and there are also some progmetal and symphonic influences to be spotted. All in all a nice and interesting mix and what is more: definitely their own sound. The strange thing is that the bio remarks that their music was inspired by seventies progressive rock and this in fact is quite far from my mind. The first three tracks are terrific songs with plenty of melody and also power because of the rather heavy guitarsound. My Aching Pain is something else again, very subdued at first, but with an orgastic, loud chorus. The song reminds me very much of a song I know, but I can't find out which. On this short album there's even a bonus track of slowed down music without a name. Probably some sonic debris.
