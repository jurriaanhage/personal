Summary

Little is known to me except that Kevin Humphrey is from the States and he does it all.

The music

I Have Walked Through The Fire sets the style of the album: a catchy rock song, maybe a bit in the vein of the commercial side of Styx. The vocals of Humphrey are okay, but not great.

Don't Want A New Lover is even more AORish, and a bit Eigthies like as well. This time the production and recording are detrimental to the song: the drumming is flat and monotonous and the vocals are mixed into the back. The bridge is quite nice, but there is no prog here. This is like Toto or Survivor, but without the financial means and a lesser performance.

Spread Your Wings is more ballad like, and indeed the melody is alright enough, but again there is no challenge to the music. For His Name's Sake shows more groove, in fact, the keyboards and guitar make for a rather full sound. The guitar sound is typically hardrock. In this song Humphrey also reveals his Christian leanings. Tears Fall From Heaven has some intense guitar playing, and Living The Future continues this line. Musically there is a break in this song which does in fact come at the right time. But the drumming doesn't really help to make the music shine.

Lion Of The Tribe Of Judah and In My Dreams do not really add anything new. The sound is rather up-beat, the lyrics to In My Dreams are very standard. Greater Is He opens with Rick Ray style guitars, while closer What Has Happened To The Truth? has a high tempo generally. The style continues to be a melodic kind of hardrock.

Conclusion

In addition to lack of good production, and the fact that a pure solo record is hard to make if a rock sound is what you are after, the content of this album is a too AOR oriented for the audience of this website. Maybe a bit of Styx here, but my main impression was one of melodic American (hard)rock. Humphrey is rather soft voiced and certainly not a screamer (which is a good thing in my opinion). But on the whole, it is all way too straightforward, too cliche, even if the melodies aren't bad at all.
