Summary

The only thing I really know about this band is that they are on Collage's label.

The music

Well the first track sounds good. Nice melody, somewhat like Collage and a bit bouncy, with a bubbling bass and the same guitar sound as Collage. The voice is sympathetic but not as good and has a lot of echo and is hard to understand. The production also leaves something to be desired.

The second one is Polish and starts out mid-tempo with some acoustic guitar. The song is rather nice, but the vocals are very much mixed into the back.

The third track is also good, with some mice melodies and Collage like guitarwork. The vocals are again a little low in the mix and maybe this is better because they aren't very strong. The song is potentially good but I have the feeling they didn't get everything out of it that they could have gotten.

In the next track the vocalist shows less restrained which might be due to the fact that he now sings in Polish. Still a lot of echo is present and the vocals still don't sound that good. The fifth track is not very interesting being rather poppy.

Fortunately the sixth is somewhat better, being a quiet lament, with some atmospherics and soft bass. The vocals sounds like he's very alone with only an echo as company.

The up-tempo follow-up has some weak moments in the middle part but picks up with some percussive playing afterwards and ends nicely with piano.

The Watchtower is a rather simple poppy track with a nice melody but not very progressive and not very interesting overall.

The next one is very good. It starts with a slow build-up, a silence before the storm which ends in a guitar solo and never fails to remind me of Eloy. Then it goes over into

My advice is: If you maintain this...er...well, just write songs like the English ones and sing Polish

The closer sounds a little like the Mission in atmosphere. It has some ethereal guitar and some flute interludes. The track is very dreamy and haunting almost.

Conclusion

Bad English on many of the tracks, so if I were them it might be best to let them be read by others or to sing in Polish. However, the English versed songs sound better than the more rocky, Polish ones. This might have happened because the Polish ones are older and this at least holds a promise for a next album. In the English tracks they sound rather like their big brother Collage, although the singing is certainly less good, but their is some good playing on this album especially on guitar. Also, a little more attention shuold be given to production; it sounds a little dull throughout. So both vocals and production could at least be done better. Best tracks are 1, 3, 9 and 10.
