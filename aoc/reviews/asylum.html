Summary

Quite some time after their debut, Longshot now return for their second sign of life.

The music

Asylum is the second in the album trilogy telling about the earth being overtaken by a terrible virus. This album's problem lies in the telling of the story, being Reese's vocals. His voice sounds somewhat husky, and rather unmusical, even to the point of outright bad in Welcome To The Mind Surgery Clinic and Armageddon. The instrumental sections are more or less flashy, with their fast keys and heavy guitars. Nothing special, but nice enough, although the drums aren't always up to scratch, being somewhat flat. This sets us up for an album that isn't quite bad, but that can hardly be called good, needing the vocal sections for variation, but being pulled down by them at the same time.

Conclusion

Longshot's debut seems to have my liking more than this one does: okay at best, definitely never good. And quite a long haul at 75 minutes.
