Summary

Another sampler from SI Music. I'll try and do this the same way as the other sampler(s).

The music

First of all I'm not familiar with the albums 10, 11 and 12 and can not give many comments on them. I must say that I do not like FAF (I used to), but this is far too commercial and uninteresting. 11 The songs starts out alright but it doesn't linger. 12 is by Clive Nolan (yet again) and it's quite mysterious and all. I saw them live and didn't like it. On album it's better. It has a bit of a Chris Isaak atmosphere (Blue Hotel...on a lonely highway), if you know what I mean in the beginning, but becomes more catchy later on. I must say that I quite like this song, but can't be raving about it.

Now about the other songs: 1. Not a very good song and the album well, it's something like it but heavier. The album is a bit metallic, but the vocals do not fit (they are female) and well, I just do not really like it. 2. A good song and the album contains some good cuts, but it's a little unbalanced. Still Rocking Horse (20 minutes) and Ghosts are hard to miss. 3. Very good song (not the best) from a very good album and as I've heard it's SI biggest seller. 4. Less than the first part, save the vocals of Alan Reed. Some pieces are good some are cheesy and so the total is unbalanced. 5. A must have. 6. My girlfriend likes this one a lot (which means it is somewhat on the softer side). I must admit that I've a soft spot for them myself and Gaia's Waltz is certainly not the best song on the album. 7. Very representative of a very good album (and live even better). A review of their newesrt is a-comin' (or has arrived). 8. Not really progressive, but melodic. This is one of the three actually good cuts. The album in it's entirety is courageous but not very interesting. 9. This song a quite heavy for the Eggies and it's certainly not the best (somewhere below the middle is a better approx.). Still, it contains some parts of other songs of the album (very very vague in the distance). A good album and well worth a try, but less progressive and more varied than their former albums.

Conclusion

For the price a good offering.
