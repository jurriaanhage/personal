Summary

Well, having asked for Ritual, daredevil me, I also asked for one of those cheap sampler. Here it is.

The music

Hmm, now I have to go over all those tracks. Oh no, wait let's just categorize these and start with the bad apples: 4 (up tempo, almost metal, although I like the way they put on something carillionish with reference to the Notre Dame), 9 (doesn't mean anything to me), 12 (too quiet and easy listening for my tastes); the okay ones: 7 (rather Bach like and kind of slow), 10 (lots of flute and a bit uneasy), 11 (lots of things going on, but doesn't strike a chord, expected better of them), 13 (nice but probably not the best thing available), 15 (started good, but got a little cheesy) and the nice or good ones, which is just about the rest: 1 (rather like Morricone and a worthwhile band anyway), 2 (I remember liking this album), 3 (veyr nice actually. Sounds a bit like Ed van Otterdyke. Who?), 5 (an oldie and rather like Eloy if you need a reference. Still not typical of them, as I remember), 6 and 14 are both rather nice and busy but with melody. Both remind me of the Steppes by Hackett. The best track surprisingly is the one by Boffo. Usually I find his music nice, but not captivating, because it's a little too friendly. This track however has some rough edges, escpecially at the end.

Conclusion

Well, the price of a thing like this is rather low, if you can get it close by. Still, to call it the best of instrumental progressive is something that you're bound to get reactions to. I mean a lot of better things exist, but most of it is rather nice and that is mostly the somewhat older stuff, while the newer stuff seems a little unbalanced and not very well composed. (At least I think things like Ivory Tower are rather new.)
