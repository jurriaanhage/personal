Summary

I saw them a few months ago in Uden and although they were very enthusiastic, but still they couldn't really captivate me. After the concert they even got to be a bit irritating during the (bad) renditions of Kate Bush songs by Tracy Hitchings. Still, I do not think any artist should make fun of another. They should know how that feels.

Their former album There's Orangie... was very much in the It Bites vein, but it couldn't really satisfy me. The album contained maybe two songs that were alright, but mostly the melodies didn't stay with me, and after a while I got bored by it. After quite a long period they are back with a new album (a variation on Songs in the Key of Life and H to He Who Am the Only One?)

The music

The album starts out alright with Placebo. I seem to remember a live rendition of this songs, but I like this better. There's less energy, but the melody is recognizable and the keyboards are not drowned out by the rest of the music. It even contains a good piano part and the vocal melody stands.

The second is far more up tempo and built on one of those riffs that I'm overly fond off. I'm not sure why, but it just doesn't catch on with me. (This is typical of a lot of the riffs on this albums.) But it's a short one, so we'll let them of the hook this time. The third song has the same problem however.

The fourth track is a long one. It's driven, very It Bites and, well, they pull it off.

The intro to track five is very cheesy and the rest of the song can't charm me either. It's very rocky with quite a few breaks, but it doesn't turn me on.

The intro to track six does not hold much promise as well. Still it turns out to be alright, although the guitar is rather tiring. The riff that goes with 'No finer way to express/exploit yourself' is very good though.

With the seventh track we get a little bit of rest...sigh...now I can just lay back and, nut ... better pay some attention to typing there.. but the song is rather boring although it's sung really well.

Like track 4 track 8 is an old song from a demo by the band. The band starts off with mandatory 'chinese music'. The song ends in an extensive keyboard/guitar jam.

The lyrics to this album are quite good; a lot better than this review. You don't have to agree with everything Dempsey says, but it's obviously not the bands stepchild.

Conclusion

Can't heartily recommend these guys. Maybe to It Bites fans although they are not up to the comparison as of yet. Still, from an interview with them that I read recently I gathered that they think that It Bites was over their head as well, so they're realistic for a start. Having seen them live, they are good technically, but one way or another it just doesn't hold my attention long enough. Maybe some of you will find their music refreshing and maybe you will enjoy their joie de vivre. This is why you can check out the songs yourself on my WWW page. Oh yeah, they have matured, because the songs do stay in your mind and the vocal melodies are better, but they're the same jumpy bunch and sometimes I like that and sometimes I don't (guess what). Best tracks are 1, 4 and 6 (more towards the end).

The booklet does learn you how to build airplane models.
