Summary

This album was sent to me by the same person who sent me Makkiwhipdies. He told me up front that it was less progressive than the latter band.

The music

This indeed seems to be the case. However this doesn't mean the music cannot be interesting. Build on a guitarriff and a nice pianic melody this is a song spiritual song about the higher self of people. The sound is rather heavy and the riff is a bit estranging and the band can be lyrically and musically compared to that other fringe progband King's X. The vocals of Blanco are soulful (like those of Living Colour and King's X) and in the higher regions he really sounds like Corey Glover. After Cage, which stylistically is like Higher Self, comes the acoustic ballad Siamese. The song has a nice, but simple melody and has this peaceful quality of a song like Under The Bridge. After a spoken intro about a circus, we come to the next rock song. The vocals are lower here and sound more aggressive, but I'm not fond of the chorus. Nude is a song about nudity and intimacy being more important than sex, while the lyrics of Last Laugh are partly in Spanish and the song starts out rather percussively. After this intro we get come to the rocking part of this track. Although the band will probably never have heard of Geoff Mann Band the melody certainly reminds me of one of Geoff Mann's tunes. A very nice track with nice percussion and more melodic and varied than the other tracks. After the low vocals of the intro of Animosity #5 that might as well introduce a rap or house track, it turns out that this is an emotional and desperate track with treated vocals and the guitar functioning as a cold blowing wind. The desperation in the vocals reminds me a little of Nine Inch Nails. What's Become Of Me is a good mid-tempo balladlike track with a great melody and sounds like how Faith No More would sound in a ballad if they'd take themselves seriously (which fortunately they don't by the way). The song even has some "choir" and bells added to make it sound a little more bombastic and I'm not one to complain about this. A great song. Love Me is quite a heavy track with definite Faith No More echoes (vocals, keyboards, double bass), but that also reminds me at times of the more aggressive Nine Inch Nails. Color Machine is dark and somber track with a subtle vocal melody. At first the accompaniment is rather sparse, but later on the drums also get themselves involved. A very subtle track that might remind some of Kravitz. Brother Blood (with ghost track) takes up the rest of the album and is industrial in approach and the most aggressive track on the album. The ghost track that is appended to this song is a good song in it's own right. It's a pianic and dreamy ballad.

The artwork, which reminds me of Boell's Ansichten Eines Clowns, but the way nicely illustrates the title of the band: a clown with a bottle of booze.

Conclusion

No bad tracks on this one, but you can judge by the references that this is not a standard progressive release. However, if you like King's X (but more varied), Faith No More (but less crazy) or Living Colour (but more melodic) you might give this band a try and if you can take a listen to Last Laugh, Animosity #5, What's Become Of Me and Color Machine. The earlier tracks are not bad, certainly not, but they are, well, more straightforward.
