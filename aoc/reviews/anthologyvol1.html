Summary

My first reaction on receiving this disc was 'who the hell is Pepe Maina?' Turns out he's an Italian who aside from releasing some solo work starting from 1978, also composed a lot for advertisements and theatre. This is a compilation with music from thirteen different albums ordered chronologically from 2002 to 1978, some of the tracks being excerpts. Most of the albums are from the past six years.

The music

It's quite noticeable that most music is meant to accompany something else that is supposed to have the main attention: the instrumental tracks are most tranquil, more often than not to the point of new ageness. The guitar work has wiffs of the work of people like Gandalf as well Andy Summers. Acceptions to this are Shoro-iiat, with Frippy guitars, Whale song, with the shuffling sound also heard in Jarre's Ethnicolor and The Wind's Game which is a rather experimental track. The final track of the album is taken from an LP, or at least, so the ticks would suggest.

Conclusion

It's remarkable how much this anthology sounds like a normal studio album. The musical style of the tracks is pretty similar, differences being just minor. It doesn't exactly show you the next best thing since sliced bread, but it's a nice collection of instrumental tracks, consistent enough to be worthwhile.
