Summary

The second album by Bert Heinen, again with the help of Marien.

The music

Hymn is the soft opener of this album. The previous album reminded me of IQ, Marillion and more yet Collage and Egdon Heath. This Egdon Heath influence I can still here in the music here. The others are certainly not directly audible anymore. Drowned is a good example: fine guitar playing, a very good vocal melody and on the whole a fine composition that reminds of nothing, is thoroughly melodious and varied. Colours Of The Summer again has a jewel of a melody. The song itself is quite dreamy and floating. Spaces OF The Deep is a more bouncy track with a key solo in the middle. Musically I think you compare Like Wendy to Timothy Purte, although the melodies of Like Wendy I can appreciate a little more. I think this is because of the more thematic approach of TP, and although Rainchild seems to be a concept album it has quite a lot of melodic variety. After the interlude I Will Find we come to the organ/key ridden Underwater Voyager. A dreamy track once again with some acoustic guitar but erupting into an emotive, slow electric guitar solo at the end. After the instrumental 1011 (binary for 11?) we come to the powerful bombast of Shadow Of The Sun, bu that ends with an atmospheric choral part. Skybound is a somewhat acoustic piece and the follow-up Four Years has a great driving guitar with ascending keyboards bringing the music to yet another climax (after Underwater Voyager). Within the same song the music transforms into a more rocky song and surprise: it works. The album closes with the easy going Wings In The Fog (guitarwork a la Collage) and the longish The River, with a powerful ending.

The booklet is very nice and well made. All dark styled.

Conclusion

Symphonic rock as it was supposed to be made. Going between dreamy and lush more atmospheric prog to the bombast of the opneing op Shadow Of The Sun to the deeply emotional guitar solo of Underwater Voyager. After the very good debut, this is one again one to cherish. If you are into melodic prog this should be for you. If you liked the debut, this one can be bought blindly.
