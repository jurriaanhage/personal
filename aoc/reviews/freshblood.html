Summary

Coming from iO Pages these guys requested that I should review them. Such a request is certainly not wasted on vain me and I gladly comply.

The music

Obviously fusion styled with the violin being the main instrument. Not as electric sounding as Jobson on Danger Money but more sounding like a real violin. In the first shortish track this already shows, with the guitar laying the heavy groundwork and the violin playing the melody. After the chapman opening the next track we get a repetitive riffing pattern on the guitar and the violin softly soloing in the back, rather free form with strong rhythms. Quite powerfully played and tension is nicely being built on this track. One has to listen quite close to hear melody with this band, but they do exist. Thief continues the drawn line, but a bit heavier maybe and some overt guitar soloing as well. Blue Screen starts out rather disjointed rhythmically while the other instruments sort of try to find their way through the song. The song is a little too frolic for my tastes. The riffing guitar is back on Missing Link though. Although in the beginning it seems to me an extended solo, later on it turns out that some progression is made during this track although somewhat slowly. The rolling thunderous sounds on this track are quite nice. The longest track is World Of Difference which is not that exciting. In the middle it dies down a little to be reawakened by a guitar solo, but not a really inspired one. Toward the end of the album a sameness creeps in. Most of the tracks have something to set them apart, but the violin is pervasive. Dragonfly contains a large dose of Chapman stick playing a repetitive riff throughout while the guitar also chips in later and the violin plays the "melody". Hypnosis is quite a heavy track with a powerful first part and better than the previous few tracks. The album ends with Fresh Blood.

Conclusion

String instrumental fusion (Ponty, but with a harder edge) with a large violin presence and usually quite worthwhile compositions and melodies. Also energetic and forceful playing, good technique but also paying attention to playing songs than showing off. However, the music tends to be rather self similar toward the end and the album sinks in a little in the middle. It might be good to do some different things as well on the next album.
