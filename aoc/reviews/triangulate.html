Summary

This is a Japanese trio, with a very special line-up (Stick and bass and no guitar or drums) and this at least their second album. No timings, since the cd would not play on my laptop. But I could review it by listening to it on an audio set, although I won't do the song one by one, also because they tend to be quite short overall.

The music

On this album we find many short tracks, with a light experimental avant-garde edge to them. The music is melodic with influence from minimal music, although occasionally one can hear elements of some of the prog bands, such as Dacoit, which has elements of ELP. The music can be cheesy at times as well, although not for very long. The music can be compared in places to the east-eurofolk of Quickion, but F.H.C. is a bit weirder and also more adventurous and proggy, as evidenced by for example Star Shape Crystal. There is no rock to speak of (happens often on Vital releases), so if that is what you are after look on. If you are kind of progperson who looks kindly toward the avant-garde, then give them a listen.
