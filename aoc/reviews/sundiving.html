Summary

Mike Fazio has been in a number of bands, the wavey Chill Faction, but also solo as Orchestramaxfieldparrish, a more recent project.

The music

Clouds Of Granite In A Clearing Sky is a big slice of ambient music, a bit wavery and hesitant at first. Then the slow, programmed percussion sets in, and we get plenty of weird, harrowing effects. But then, something akin to music emerges from underneath. The song has plenty of fiddling around with knobs the like, and thus never really gets underway, and it is also a bit too wayward to dream away on. Around the fifteen minute mark, the music is markedly friendlier, and we get some runs on piano and such. But behind it all, we hear the needle of a record player going, and the interlude is relatively short: the monotonous rhythms soon return. Around the twenty-two-minute mark, the sound darkens, and maybe they could have left the really jarring piece out this time, because I finally got the impression things were moving to a head. The closing section is full of space.

The Whole Electric City In Front Of Us is only a minute or two and does not bring us much new. Slick-O-Phonic is a rather percussive and groove piece, but it lacks appeal for me. The rhythms are modern and monotonous, the keyboards ambient, with a bit of didjeridoo style sounds thrown in. Heard a minute and you heard it all. Rhythm stay important on The Sound You Make When You Reach For Tomorrow, which is a title the music does not live up to. A bit too much like euro dance for my tastes. Sundiving does not bring us anything new.

Conclusion

Compared to earlier works by Mike Fazio I heard, this is the one I like the least. Too much monotony to the rhythms for one, and the music is a bit too restless to take on as ambient. The long opener has its moments, but after that I lost interest quickly. Can't win 'em all.
