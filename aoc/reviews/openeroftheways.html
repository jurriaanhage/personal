The music

Making a living isn't easy, if you're in electronic music. The apparent every day struggle for survival of the late eighties and early nineties has brought Steve Hillman to the recycling of instruments. This album here is dominated by sequencer music, thus strongly reminding of the works of Tangerine Dream of the second half of the seventies. Alongside with this we find a fuzzy guitar reminiscent of Gandalf's early works. And to top it of, Steve got himself a drumbox that sounds as unnatural as possible, which was mixed rather to the front in a number of tracks. Sadly, the liner notes tell us the drumbox on this 2001 re-recording is Steve himself. But seriously... this album doesn't sound all that bad, but I'd rather put on an old TD album, since they at least were using technology that was new then. Now all this stuff sounds very outdated, even if you consider its original recording period.
