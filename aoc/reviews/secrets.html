Summary

Over the years Swedish band Cross have released their fair share of CDs. This is their 2000 release Secrets.

The music

My problem with this band traditionally is that their compositions aren't all that interesting. You can imagine my surprise when I found that this album did contain some pretty nice stuff. There are still moments that aren't quite to my liking, but there does appear to be a tendency to improve in this band.

Conclusion

Cross appear to be getting better, however, their compositions remain somewhat plodding, and Cross' vocals remain an acquired taste. This is a better album than what they released before this, but ample room for improvement remains.
