Summary

They have one album out, but I've never heard it, so let's get to the music quickly, because I'm curious myself.

The music

The album starts out quite relaxed with piano and laid back vocals. The vocals are alright, but not good. It can be gathered from the song lengths that this is a regular progressive release and it is.

In the first track enough tempo changes and surprisingly the track reminds me in the melodic parts of Brave (the album, the organ and the vocal melody to be precise). The song does not flow as easily as does the whole of Brave and there are some interludes thrown in as well with some Genesis references.

Still, I would like to draw the main comparison with Galahad, both in vocals (also a bit high and well, sharp?) as in build up and approach. Overall, the tracks are likeable, but not very rememberable and this is at once my main point of criticism. I mean I can listen to stuff like this without any problem, but the problem is that it's not captivating and lasting. Often enough the tracks do not seem to go anywhere. I do have to remark that every song contains some worthwhile and good parts, but as whole it just doesn't sum up to a good album.

Notable exceptions to this criticism are Eye Next to Glass, Late of Conscience and the closing epic The Final Stroke. The first is a soft and mysterious track and the latter two are characterised by a good build up and good melodies with enough changes to satisfy anyone.

Conclusion

Typical progressive rock, well executed, but without much lasting value. I do think a lot neo-progressive listeners will like this album, but it's hard to love it. A liking to a band like Galahad is a great help there. The best tracks are Eye Next to Glass, Late of Conscience and The Final Stroke. The latter two very eventful, good melodies and convincingly brought. There is hope, because these songs add up to almost half the album.
