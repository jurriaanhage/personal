Summary

The music

Aeronautics is the second release by Jorn Lande band Masterplan. Even more so than on the debut they display a slick, rather commercial sounding, rendition of what one might call prog metal. The tracks are firm, especially on the guitar and Lande's somewhat hoarse vocals. The use of synths, and the almost artificial sounding drums take off any edge there might be. This combined with the open compositions (with nicely rhyming lyrics such as "I was drinking, never thinking"), one would have to say that the music is more pop using a stronger instrumental idiom. This comes most clearly to the attention in the apparent single Back For My Life. I guess late eighties Magnum would be a decent enough reference. Wounds and closer Black In The Burn show some Rhapsody semblances.

Conclusion

This second release will probably go down easily with those who liked the more accesible side of the debut. The tracks are radio friendly, even more so than with the previous disc. The album should appeal to rather wide audiences, but I must say that I find what is presented here is too glib and unoriginal to draw my attention. In becoming attractive to wider audiences I feel the band have become uninteresting for prog metal fans. The previous one just stayed inside boundaries, but I know that they have lost me on this one.
