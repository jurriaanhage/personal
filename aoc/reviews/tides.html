Summary

The second CD of this Dutch band. After their first Nightlines they got a contract with SI to release the CD lying here before me.

The music

Neo-prog at its best I would say, they are in line with bands like Marathon and For Absent Friends and I'd rate the the latter as the least interesting, though I haven't heard their last one.

Good melodies, good playing, good singing and emotions all are present on this one album. Again, note that this is neo, songs about down to earth affairs, lots of guitar, with keys figuring in the background. There's enough variety to warrant the progrock stamp, so no complaining about that either.

As the songs are really songs and there are no epics about I'll skip the song by song description this time and just note that the album contains a number of up-tempo, mid-tempo songs and ballads and combinations of these. The singer has a voice that reminds me of the singer of Ten Sharp but less nasal. Now about some of the songs.

The songs are on first listen quite alike. The guitars are prominent as well as the vocals and all other instruments come on the second spot. Some are ballads, like _The Spy_ (with some mad sax by Erwin Werring) and _Where are you?_, some are instrumentals _BBC_ (using some organ) and _Waiting_ but most songs are built on the guitarriffs played by Ron van Kruistum. I think the keyword to the music is involving, because like with some of the older Marillion songs (Chelsea Monday), you get pulled into the song.

Conclusion

A very worthwhile second CD by this Dutch band and well worth a try for fans of neo prog.
