The music

Fortunately this album is not very progrock, since otherwise I would have needed to come up with alphabetical alternatives to the cyrillic titles and names on this album. The contents varies between acoustic guitar dominated pop songs, a few introspective (e.g. track 9) and a few orchestral tunes, and some heavier excursions with strong rhythm guitar work (track 4 and 7). The songs themselves are fine, often with good melodies, and there is nothing wrong with the sound and the production either. In places, like track 3, 6 and 11, the music is a bit more thoughtful and atmospheric. The vocals are all in Russian, although the final song is an electronic instrumental. A professional release, but in view of the style, which has little to do with progrock, not a likely candidate for your wish lists.
