Summary

Started in 1993, this is the fourth cd single of this that band from Holland, which was heretofore unbeknownst to myself.

The music

The "single" opens this EP. It shows a band playing melodic music with a large role for the atmosphere building keyboards. The music is quite moody and Rebecca's vocals are quite okay on this track. Do not expect complicated music though, since this is just melodic poprock and nothing more or less. Fairy Tale does not add anything to this and is in fact a rather boring track with some propositions removed to not only make it fit the music, but change the semantics as well. She's The One is a plain rock track with a riff nicked from somewhere and a very poppy unimpressive chorus. Dark is somewhat better with the singer reciting a la Anne Clarke (not so depressed though) and a rather nice melody (the often repeated one). Again thoroughly laced with keyboards. Cooldawn again adds nothing to what has been done save maybe some Curish basswork. After a nice pianic reprise of Mother we come to the closer a live track: After The Party. In addition to worse soundquality the vocalist shows some emotion on this track in her somewhat desperate highpitched vocals.

Conclusion

Certainly not up to par with current melodic rock bands such as FAF, Marathon and PTS this is a band that should delve deeper and put more power into their music. This is made explicit by the higher energy level of the live track, although the composition is not the better for it.
