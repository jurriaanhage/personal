Summary

Silent Voices is a Finnish prog metal band, around since 1995. After self releasing a single (of which b-side HumanCradleGrave made this album) this is their album debut.

The music

Silent Voices' sound is an open one, displaying lots of key, but also with rather sharp guitar solos. The tracks are generally well put together. The vocals are not vintage, but above average within this musical vein. For one thing, Henneken generally manages to keep from shouting and hollering. The background vocals sometimes have a Europe like quality (notably so in Tragedy) I could have done without. The compositions show the band capable of creating tension fitting both long and short tracks, without excelling, save for the climax of Glassheart. Like so often, this album has a dip in the mid range: tracks 4 and 5 fall short of the level of the other material, as does closer The Last Sunset. There are no clear influences to the music of Silent Voices, but the way they use keys and guitars would put them in the Dream Theater category of prog metal.

Conclusion

Chapter Of Tragedy is an album of solid prog metal, which without sounding unique, stands out on its own. Best track is Glassheart.
