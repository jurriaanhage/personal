Summary

Well, real progheads will have seen that this album also contains a cover of VDGG Meurglys III that was also featured on Eyewitness. IMO this was the best cover of Eyewitness so that should hold a promise for the rest of the album, doesn't it? Translation of the title by the way is ...And His Breathing Is Still Moving The Waves.

The music

If you think that making a good VDGG cover makes it obvious that the band is VDGG fanatic, think again. Although some references to Meurglys III can at least be found in Malcreanza, this is yet another one of those free style Italian prog CDs. On this album the shorter tracks are usually acoustic ones, very melodic and sometimes decorated with some sampled spoken text. Most of the other tracks are long and rather varied. This however doesn't say that for instance 1e Maggio and Avant-Grado tend to sound alike a little.

So, what more can they offer. Well, 1e Maggio for instance starts out acoustically but at a point becomes very hectic. The piano playing is rather quirky and free style and after some more melodic stuf on flute and organ wthe hectic part returns. It all ends with a jazzy piano. Putting as much as this into one track does make it sound a little disjointed however.

The third one is more a whole I think. It begins rather frantic, but after a very memorable piano riff and some rough guitarplaying it tends to the psychedelic and ends abruptly.

The forceful beginning on keyboards to Le Onde are over the top (but good) and they take do switch to lower gear soon after. A lot of breaks and more forceful pieces on this track. Still, it sounds more like jamming than decent songwriting to me.

A good track is Avant - Grado with an acoustic start, a good vocal melody, a little on the mysterious side (but we don't mind that) and some good busy organ stuff. Still in this track uit sounds a lot more meaningful than before.

Conclusion

Another release with a few good tracks (2, 3 and 9) and the few resting places in between. Still in some of the songs I get the impression that not eveyrthing is well thought through and not very meaningful. Just a string of nice ideas.
