Summary

Nowadays no-one would dare appear on an album cover like they did, seemingly a bunch of Peter Gabriels. Anyway we here to appreciate the music, so lets.

The music

The first track is like it says, more or less an intro, showcasing the various instruments in varying melodies. The first reminds me a bit of Soft Machine, while the later remind me more of a band like Kayak, more poppy in approach.

The next track starts out like a lullabye, with sweet harmonies, reminding me of Yes, although the harmonies are not THAT good. Especially the second part of this track reminds me a lot of Yes, (Siberian Khatru). The third part is less so however, and the lullabye returns after which we move into some hectic jazzy organplaying waters.

Obelisk is again old progressive with the first part is rather rhythmic and quirky with a lot of different percussion involved and meandering keyboardsolo's. The second part Hora Nata has a very good melody, a bit in the vein of Focus, flowing and rousing. The next part is rather abstract with keboards and percussion and after a while the guitar sets in for the next part.

Hattrick starts out with keyboards, and after a passionate and well played guitar solo returns to the quirky intro. Then the bass is allowed to get into the fore, after which one can hear some violin (is it an imitation or not?) and then the guitar starts it psychedelic trip.

Three Magicians enjoys a quiet pianic start, with a somewhat wavery voice. Halfway the first part it gets more involved, with some rapid percussion and piano. It ends with a heavy guitarsolo. The next part starts again rarther softly. This part is very delicate and has a good melodic build-up, which starts on piano but is continued on guitar (Focus). In the piece Bernst it picks up speed, with some quick marimba. It ends in a well-sung ballad, majestically.

The sixth track is Hora Nata again as released on single, before the album. Rather angelic, slow and mesmerizing.

Good morning life again owes something to Yes, again mostly due to the vocals. The song is rather optimistic. For the rest of the tracks: We're so sorry is very poppy and not very good, Be Careful Now is rather angelic and can be compared to Illusion, Talking About My Love is a rather naive love song, and the closer On The Rocks is not so interesting. The six bonustracks are more accessible than the album tracks.

Conclusion

All in all a rather outdated work, with a lot of variation and different melodies, quirky but in all not very meaningful. The sound is good and they've taken trouble to include some singles as well, as we have reason to expect from Pseudonym. By the way, this CD contains everything the band ever made. This album is interesting for those that love old progressive with a lot of organ, old keyboard sounds, jamming guitars, quirky rhythms and lots of breaks. Should be enough of those around. Still, even in his genre it is not that good. The music is nice but with me it just doesn't stick. References that come to my mind most easily are Kayak, for the mood, Focus, for many of the guitarsolo's, and mostly Yes, especially for the vocal parts. A band with many faces.
