The music

Autumn opens with sustained keyboards, after which the melodic music sets in rather peacefully: tinkling bell like sounds, darker synths in the back as accompaniment and then the guitar sets in also rather quietly. The melody played reminds me a bit of King Crimsons Starless. The music continues to be quite pastoral with piano and whispered vocals and a strongly melodic approach, with repeatedly alternating parts. What strikes me is the sparseness of the music. Most obvious reference is Anthony Phillips, but without the sophistication. The quiet atmosphere stays in Earth And Stone, a track that seems to lie slowly basking in the sun. All a bit too uneventful for me. The song is mostly built on some harmonica like keyboards and quiet organs in the back. The second part is minimal piano music. Nice to listen to. The rather short Indian Summer is mainly acoustic guitar and this is continued in Amnesty. The electric guitar here is used in a good way: a very clear sound. After a few minutes we get a little pace and the song becomes slightly folky with a nice, somewhat merry melody. Landfall is with over eleven minutes the longest track on the album and it does not bring anything new. The music is again rather repetitive and slowly evolving. Synthetic flute and piano on this one for the most part. The synthetic brass later on, does not really help. Towards the end I got quite a scare when the keyboards suddenly sounded quite sharp. Sunlight On Leaves is a rather sharp little pieces with pointed guitarwork on a soft bed of acoustic guitar. In this way Andrews nicely evokes the both the softness of the leaves and the sunshine as well as the blinding aspect of it. The last track is a bit of demo quality so its status of bonus track is deserved. It sounds very familiar, but I can't place it exactly. The music is quite percussive, but still rather slow. It has a Southamerican feel to it.

Conclusion

An album that could not impress me. I can't say I don't like it or anything, but is all so quiet and repetitive that is just doesn't do much to me. There's some beauty here, in the melodies most oftenly, but the music is both a bit too friendly as well as uneventful.
