Summary

A band from Germany, but I guess you could have told from the member's names.

The music

Again some neo in my review (does he review anything else?). Tea for Two is a German band (you might have guessed that from the names) and they make quiet almost romantic and thoroughly dramatic melodic rock. Most notable, though not necessarily good) on this CD are the lovesongs (Way Of Life, Time Of Passion for instance) for which the singer has a voice that is on the hand dramtic, but on the other hand might be deadly for most of you listeners, because this guy has got a nasty accent. He singing is at times a bit like squeaking. I get used to things like this mostly, but not everyone does so be warned.

Like I said, most of the songs are kind of quiet and I must admit that the acoustic songs are worthwhile (Temporale, Remember Jermaine and Time Of Passion). Sometimes the flute is quite pronounced and thus evokes Jethro Tull and Red Jasper. Other passages that I found familiar reminded me of the beginning of Creepshow and the Perimeter edge-part of Misplaced Childhood.

Conclusion

The strong points of this CD are the drama, the melodies and the acoustic songs. Negative comments I do have on this album: the vocals aren't good (they are acceptable, but nothing beyond that), the whole sounds kind of amateuristic and a bit bland (probably because of the production, it's home made), not so much variation on account of the songs, very subdued. Most notable influences are Chandelier and Red Jasper.
