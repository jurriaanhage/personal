Summary

Debut album by this British band from Ramsgate (Kent).

The music

Released on SI's sublabel Silly Insects, they bring us a collection of songs that are mostly in the It Bites vein, a type of music some people do not regard as progressive. This kind of music is supposed to be fresh and energetic(which brings me to the other related band: Ark). Still Summer Indoors does not please me like Ark does. Ark is a lot more versatile, varied and is compositionswise richer. The songs on this album are hard to differentiate between at first listen. They do succeed in being energetic, but only rarely do they really grasp the listener by the throat and scream into his ear: Well, here I am, listen to me you bastard!! and well, I guess that's what this music should really be about, with being energetic, lots of breaks and an omnipresent rhythmsection and heavy guitars and the keyboards a bit lagging behind. Positive exceptions to the rule are Dogs in Space and Peak Time Boulevard. Though most songs have heir moments. I'm missing songs with identity and so for the rest it's mostly nice but not great. Worth of notice are the lyrics though, that really seem to have subjects that are also of importance in our current world (a lot of those bands like Ark and It Bites seem to have that too).
