The music

This mini disc is sort of a preamble for SSS's soon to be released full debut album. The band exhibit the same kind of seriousness The Presidents of the USA do, without wanting to compare the bands any further. The opener is sort of punky, the second is not really musical, but the thirth and especially the fourth track show why we, being a progressive web site, got this disc: the stuff is sort of reminiscent of King Crimson's instrumental workings. Pretty good stuff. I wonder how the full album is gonna turn out, whether it's going to be good stuff with a humoristic touch, or nonsense with a musical touch. This demo disc shows the potential for both.
