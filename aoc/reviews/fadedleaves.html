Summary

Despite the suggestion of its name, this is a Greek band, presenting their third release here, after albums from 1996 and 1999.

The music

The album opens with the well construed Silence. Good vocals, fittingly, though sparsely, accompanied with just the right amount of tension. The following Castle On The Sand also tries to balance sparseness and tension, but misses out partially. The just a little too often repeated title doesn't help either. Unfortunately this second track is a better indicator for the rest of the album than the first is. Most tracks have their moments, with nice key sections and (mostly) strong vocals, but do not contain enough ideas to justify their length.

Conclusion

La Tulipe Noir could probably have made a good forty, maybe fifty minute album. Unfortunately they chose to make a 68 minute one, spreading their, in themselves strong enough, ideas just a little too thin for comfort. And therefore this has become an album you wouldn't particularly fancy listening to as a whole. Silence, Castle On The Sand and Le Fond Du Ciel are the best tracks.
