Summary

The music

Carbon 7 and Pierre Vervloesem are known names within the avant prog part of this world. John Litton Baroi, however, sounds just a little bit different. Judging from the music it was Baroi who decided what this album sounded like. Most of the tracks are of a rather ethnic sounding kind. Often those are enhanced by a bit of modern rhythmn brought in. Avant prog is far from us in most tracks. Tor Hashite is rather laidback and has some subtle effects, making it rather listenable. A track like Kal Gelo sounds pretty bouncy and irritating. Positive exception is Pothiker Vvhari Bozha having some instrumental, guitar led sections which sound a lot more progressive, even though embedded in ethnic sounds and vocals.

Conclusion

Sure, carbon 7 have been known to stretch the idea of avant prog, some odd releases by these guys came by. This disc, however, is too far off base for the label. A number of the tracks with their hackneyed rhythmns browbeat any hint of authenticity. Another bunch have a more traditional ehtnic sound, and are more listenable, but apart from a bit of guitar lack a progressive approach. This adds to an album that has no real progressive interest.
