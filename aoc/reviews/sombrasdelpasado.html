Summary

This is Ankhara's third album.

The music

Genesis, fitting its name, is the beginning, an overture.

Ruinas Del Alma is a combination of strong guitars, in a rather metal-like vein, and clear sounding piano. The vocals are a tad on the raw side, but not irritatingly so. The end result is pretty much prog metal like.

Acordes Magicos is more up tempo, bordering on hard rock. Pretty straight stuff, with several freaky guitar solos, drowning out the keys. Not very interesting.

Busca Un Motivo makes more room for keys once again, but the melody is still pretty straight forward, as is the rhythm. AOR stuff with some extra guitaristic strength.

The next two tracks are somewhat power metallish, drama, both in vocals as in guitar and synths, with constant rhyhtm guitar. Not bad, but not interesting either.

Un Dia En La Imaginacion starts off pretty rocky, but the synths later on attempt to make up for this.

Principio Y Fin is a bit on the sappy side, quiet vocals with a bit of a jazzy piano solo.

Mirame kicks up the speed again, highly melodic, choppy drumming. El Mundo... has a lot of guitar and rock in it. Neither gets my attention.

Sombras Del Pasado has a bit more of a build up, with quiet almost dreamy sections, but also some dynamics. This is pretty progressive in overall feel, and with that one of the more interesting tracks on the album.

Conclusion

Ankhara manages to mix up several rock oriented styles on this album. This in itself is a nice feat. Having said that, this album becomes largely uninteresting for progressive fans by that approach. Stereotypically, the two longest tracks on the album are the most progressive, and with that most interesting. To me, anyway. The players are obviously able enough in playing both classical and rock oriented sections, but the general musical vein is too far outside my -and this site's- field of focus.
