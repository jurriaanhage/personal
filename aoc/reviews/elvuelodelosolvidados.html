Summary

This is another re-release by Viajero Inmovil. In the past I've given some cynical commentary on the level of such releases. This one proves to be better than a number.

The music

The general demeanor of Canturbe's work is rather mellow, almost acoustic progressive material, in the style of Italian seventies progressive. This is demonstrated very clearly early on in a way appealing enough, and of definite interest to those into the style. The third track is another track in the style, but misses the conviction of the first two, insecure vocals and pretty plain compositionally.

The longer track En Virtud Del Obcecado incorporates some jazzrock material, quite akin to Santana's seventies work: fast guitar with clear salsa-like influences. A nice break from what might have become too self similar. From this we pick it up where we left with Suenos De Ayer: acoustically oriented stuff that is pretty decent most of the time, sounding too thin at others.

Conclusion

I've criticized Viajero Inmovil re-releases before. Most of the material on this disc is seventies Italian progressive style. Some of the tracks are pretty thin, far from convincing, and the sound remains too gentle to rattle anyone. Considering the fact that this album wasn't originally released until 1980, it can hardly be called original. Still, the overall sound is not so bad.
