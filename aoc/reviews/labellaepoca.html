Summary

The music

Yet another re-release by Viajero Inmovil. This album contains pretty acoustically driven early seventies progressive, as was made more often in latin countries at the time. Unfortunately most compositions aren't particularly compelling, having a somewhat sedate feel to them. The vocals are laid back at best, but a little too often miss out, by insecure, off key (Ella Es Tu Hermana has some pretty bad moments), or both.

Conclusion

The lover of the Italian style progressive might want to consider this album. As far as I'm concerned this album has its moments, but they are too few and too far between to matter, especially if your consider that the stuff inbetween can be sort of hard to sit through.
