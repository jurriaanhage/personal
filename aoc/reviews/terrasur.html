Summary

They played at progfest, that much is true and I know the bunch is from the U.S.A., but that's all. They do have another album out I think.

The music

Talk about varied songlengths! The music turns out to be rather "experimental". A lot of weird sounds and very long and very short tracks make listening an interesting experience. The first track is meant as an intro, but I wouldn't say that this is life as we know it. The track is a bit tribal like with a lot of rhythm and some jungle sounds like screaming monkeys and so on. Comparable in atmosphere to say Robert Wyatt on Rock Bottom. Very slow.

The next track shows more of the bands nature. It starts out in mid-tempo with a good guitar solo and afterwards a bit dated keyboard solo and tempo and signature changes. The vocals are moody, sleepy and subdued; drawling is the correct word, I think. If you know Castanarc you should know what I mean. If you sing like this you have to able to write some very exciting tracks and although the song contains some good melodies, the composition is a little too unstructured. Still, an intriguing track.

The other tracks bring us more or less more of the same. Atmospheric music, often very sad and depressed with the soft and sometimes whining voice of McFarland. The music is sometimes very melodic and flowing and at times gives way to Tangerine Dream like meandering synth solos. These can be quite unaccessible. The Philosophy track is a collection of strange sounds and is used to break the monotony really of the other tracks. Not that you get bored, but most of the tracks share that desolate feeling of open windy, snowy fields and no hope of reprieve. Very sad. The booklet suggests deserts, but open icy spaces suits the music better.

The rhythm section is quite alright, very varied, but the drums sound a bit dry. The sound of the band is enriched by for instance the sounds of whales in Neptune's Last Tear that is the best track of this album.

The album closes with the very strange ...The end of life as we know it. Lots of soundeffects here.

Conclusion

Well, you really have to be into this kind of music. Personally it reminded me a bit of Floyd atmospherics with the samples and also the atmosphere of Not Drowning Waving. The vocals may be a little dull for some, but if you can, give them a chance as something different from most of the song oriented prog of today.
