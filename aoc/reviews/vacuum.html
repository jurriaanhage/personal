Summary

A trio from Spain, who have been recording since 1992.

The music

Opening with applause to themselves the first of the tracks already indicates what we might expect in the coming: the music is free form, hardly touchable in places, with vocals tortured, and at times totally a-melodious. Actually I don't really like these vocals, and I'm afraid people will have this problem. King Crimson is often mentioned with this band, but I think the experimentations of Antropophobia go quite a bit further. The music is more in the RIO vein (recent Present for instance). The productional quality is rather low, but this kind of music does not hinge on that. Salvation Is To Build is a more concrete track, rocky, with rather a lot of variation in signature and melody (there are melodies here). Again the vocals are for me the weakest part of this track. The music by the way settles in quite well with progmetal here with heavy drums, rhythm guitars and the like. If I Could Reign is to acoustics and strained vocals and harmonies and rolls on the drums. I do wonder where the marimba comes from on this track. Window is the next one up. Again lots of variation rhythm and melody wise and if you like your breaks plenty and fast, this is alright. The song has a subdued quality nonetheless with a weird combination of light acoustic guitar and at the same time long heavy chords on electric guitar. Hti (whatever it my mean) continues the line of complexity with some more aggressive vocals stylings and alternation between quiet and busy passages. The music never gets really heavy, but this is partly a production thing. Very melodic bass playing on this track. I like it. The final part is busy and varied drumming with melodic acoustic guitar and the singer softly wailing in the back. The Spanish influence comes through strongly in the acoustic guitar part. Mines is a short track one with all the variation compressed into a minute or three. Out Of This World features the now well-knwon ingredients: free drumming, the loose singing, acoustic guitar and even some "keyboards" in the background. One of the more memorable track is Ignorance with its slow mysterious passages and the low guitarwork. Diabolus In Voce is just some people talking and after that we get four of the tracks of the in repeat, but in different versions. The album closes with some more talking. To be honest I have no idea whether these songs are part of the album as such. As for the difference with the originals, the vocals sound further away and it might be that these tracks were recorded live. The vocalist sounds a bit more powerful and melodic here, still not very strong (but I think this is also a question of production), but not so strangely wrung out as on the record proper. The music is a bit more heavy and better defined. After some careful inspection I concluded that these songs were the ones present on If I Could from 1998. I do not know whether these songs are officially part of Vacuum.

Conclusion

This is not easy music. Certainly not. Lots of variation, but compositionally these guys do tend to pull it off. What is still lacking is in production, execution (which I feel could be more fluent in a way) and for the most part in the vocal regions. It may be that these vocals are such as they are on purpose but to my ears, the singer just can't sing, his vocals is so wavering and unsteady. In the lower regions it is a bit better, but the vocals are still not good enough. Musically the band is quite interesting, although they take some time to appreciate. They put a lot of variation into their music, but for some reason it comes off quite well and did not strike me as superfluous. Also the introduction of acoustic guitar (played in this style) is rather new. Comparisons can be made with RIO, King Crimson and Gordian Knot.
