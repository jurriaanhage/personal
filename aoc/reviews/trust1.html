Summary

The music

The artwork of this album spells American alt. Looks can deceive: Adult Nuthouse is a Finnish duo that makes music that would best have been released on the Shimmy Disc label. This stuff is pretty much avant garde, in the literal meaning: against the grain, although there are semblances to wave and industrial stuff at times as well. The singing is more vocal than it is musical, and the use of other instruments in general is not as you would expect it to be. From both artwork (the two WTC towers in a desert) and lyrics it appears that the duo is trying to make a bunch of points, typically by cynically discribing what they -I presume- think is wrong on the one hand, being totally ridiculous on the other, to the point of You Bitch being of a South Park like bluntness.

The result is -what a surprise- a rather odd album. It is in not what you would call progressive (barring the one off Pere Ubu resemblance). However, those who lean towards a love for experimentalism might find a number of interesting moments in this album. I like part of this album, as much as some of it irritates me. I can't help feeling, though, that there's just a little too much experiment and a little too less music on this one. For those of you listening to the samples: the strange volume variations on Lemmy Caution aren't caused by bad sampling...

Conclusion

Steer away from this when you're looking for melodicism, but when you're into oddballs and experiments you could give this a try.
