Summary

The debut of this weirdish English band.

The music

The cover of the album, I did in fact review this album from vinyl, the version in which I got it, is a nice one by R.H. Giger (yep the one of the Brain Salad Surgery and quite a few other covers). The band has two definite influences. The first of these are those loonies the Cardiacs: large numbers of tempo changes and the fact that the music is a hasty kind of symphonic rock. The Cardiacs however have the tendency to fill up the rest with ska and PEH does the same with psychedelic rock in the style of Hawkwind and the Ozrics. This is felt as a result of the spacey guitar and keyboard playing, like what can be found on What Ya Gonna Be Son? Lyrically the band takes on the generation gap (What Ya Gonna Be Son?) genetic manipulation (the varied The Unborn), the Middle-East (the more accessible and driven Creature Feature) and the end of the world in general (Twentieth Century Man/President's Reply). Complete with a number of short synthinstrumentals and noises, this has become a well-sounding and played album.
