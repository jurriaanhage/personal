Summary

A follow up to Tus Oidos Mienten.

The music

Tus Oidos Mienten was half good, because some the tracks lacked direction and left me wanting. The music on this album is at least similar in style, being a kind of noisy psychedelic underground rock. The beginning of the first track also lacks the direction necessary to make music interesting, but during the track it gets better. The middle part is again noisy and very fast, but after that we get into a part which is almost Oldfieldian in sound, repetitive acoustic guitar playing. Nice touch. Then the tempo picks up again

The following track has a very clear guitarsound at the start, but at some point the track speeds up and the guitarsound is again noisy and heavy. Rather like Hendrix but a little heavier.

The next track is overall quite friendly although some menacing guitarparts are thrown in. This track even has some spacy Floydian guitarwork to be admired. The tempo is low and the melody is good with a nice build-up.

The fourth track is dedicated to Frank Zappa (well, they're his initials, right?). A spacy track with some acoustic intermezzo's, but not very interesting. Just a lot of sound.

The longest track in the beginning plods along until we come to a mysterious intermezzo. After that it's back to the plodding track. On the whole the track is thus rather slow and repetitive and majestic in a dark sort of way.

Gong is a short acoustic track, while Nederweit (almost Nederwiet, the Dutch word for a kind of Dutch soft drugs) is spacey and at the start rather cosmic. After a minute or so the distorted guitar sets in while at the end the track becomes quite neurotic.

Conclusion

Again a CD full of noisy, dark, distorted progressive guitarrock a la Hendrix but going a little further (these are the nineties right?) and there's also some spacerock involved. The sound is more varied than on Tus Oidos Mienten and there's are some nice melodies as well to be admired.
