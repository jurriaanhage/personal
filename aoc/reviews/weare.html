Egoband - We Are...

Artist: Egoband
Title: We Are...
Label: Mellow Records MMP 282
Length(s): 60 minutes
Year(s) of release: 1995
Month of review: 04/1996

Line up

Jacopo Giusti - drums, percussion
Alfonso Capasso - bass
Simone Coloretti - guitars, e-bow

Summary

Egoband did a relatively good cover of the difficult track Arrow on the Eye-witness compilation. Wonder what they are like in real life.

The music

After a cliche start of this album with the intro Energy Goes On, We Are ... kicks off fluently with a lot of keys. The vocals as it turns out are rather bad, but acceptable IMO, but maybe not for others. The lyrics are very vague and remind of Eloy lyrics. The music is kind of rocky, but can be very spacey as well. It turns out that much of the music on this album is rather spacey and they certainly like to jam at times. Unfortunately this is not always for the best. A few minutes into the thrid track I'm definitely reminded of the poet recital on Misplaced Childhood and the atmosphere reminds me of Hotel Hobbies. After a while it becomes very spacy again. Not like Hawkwind, not that much rock, but more free space rock. A meandering guitar with a layer of synths beneath it.

The next song is entirely different, being a very sad ballad about Lost Friends. The voice of the singer is well used here. It continues into an acoustic ditty, 5.

After these softer notes, we continue with a heavy organ and all round heavy playing in So Far Away, which is blessed by a good vocal melody and reminds me, if it should remind me of anything, of KC.

The seventh track is again reasonably free styled. I mean that there is little structure to the song and it is as a whole rather quiet although there's an occassional outburst.

The next track is one of the best on the album. It is rather bombastic and starts hectic with some mellotron thrown in and has some good melodies. In the middle there's an a-capella part and afterwards we have some piano and drums keeping it also rather soft and even some jazzy piano playing After more mysterious, threatening part, there's some good guitarwork, played with a lot of feeling.

Aside from a little ELP in the closer it is mid-tempo Gabriel like track.

Conclusion

Not something I can heartily recommend. The vocals aren't for one not good enough and some of it is rather sloppy (or free style, which ever term you like best). Anyway, this might be something for lovers of space rock (aside from the ballas maybe). Best tracks are 3 and 8 (funny is that 8 is the reprise of 3) Strange however that in some parts the vocals work very well and in others makes you wish for better.
