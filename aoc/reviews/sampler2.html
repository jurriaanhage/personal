Summary

The second sampler concerning three groups of albums. The first is the most interesting for my own chauvinist heart: it concern 'classic' Dutch progressive albums by obscure artists (so not Kayak, Supersister etc.). The songs in question are 1, 2, 3, 8, 9 and 14. 14 is a special one because I already have a strange copy of it, but as a demo CD for some new remastering and packaging line for CDs. I will not concern myself with 14. for now, because the entire CD will be reviewed as well somewhere else by me. The second class is very much related to the former, because it also concerns rereleases, but this time of albums that have already been released on CD. Only 11 and 12 fall into this category. The other songs are from albums that are not made for SI directly but have been released by them on Pat.Pending or on the Silly Insects label (you can find out for yourself which songs these are).

The music

First of all the songs that I did not already know: 4,5 and 13. Nr. 4 is by a reviewer of SI with the help of singer Jens van der Stempel (former singer of Egdon Heath) and I must admit that it makes me curious about the rest of the album, because it sounds very good and the singing is great. The song reminds me at times of Vopos on Stationary Traveller by Camel, in melody mostly. Song 5 is by Genesis copy-cat Citizen Cain. Very easily the influences of Genesis are found in this song (that I must admit has it's charms). The last song is not much to my liking. It's very neo, and I do not like the vocals on this song. Some for Lorien, but maybe there album is a bit better. The harmonies at the start do remind me of Now (or Yes for that matter).

I think the best thing is to say how well the other songs reflect the qualities of the respective albums: 1. quite a good song this (of course they couldn't put the 20 minute title track on this sampler). It contains just about everything Differences has to offer and I must admit that it's a lot better than their later release A World of Diff. 2. In the City is also a good album and the titletrack contains a lot you can expect from the rest of the album. As always the keyboards are the best part of the song and I must admit that I'm not too happy with the poppy refrain. Not the best song on the album (Secret Fence for instance is a lot better), but very satisfying. 3. Coda also made one good album and this song is very representative of it. 6. Is quite a bombastic song, by Barrett sing-a-like Robert Reed (see somewhere else for the review of his latest). For King and Country was a reasonable album and this is quite a good song from it. Still, not an album that I'd recommend without reservations. This problem with the album is that the songs are a bit unbalanced, due to poppy influences. 7. This song is one of the highpoints of the CD, so if this is not your idea of a good song forget it. They are in the It Bites and Ark area, but not as good. 8. Arkus: this song has it all and says it all. I have the idea that this song is actually all you need from this album, because the rest is so similar to it (it's a concept album so that might be the reason). It's good though. 9. YWis are a very early neo-prog band and though their lyrics are not really up to it, they have at times some fantastic guitar and keyboard work and really worth listening to. The song is quite representative of the album, but forget about the lyrics. 10. A ballad by 'heavy grunge' band Mercy Train. Not the best song on the album and not really representative either, because most of the rest is heavier. The album is worth a try though, but try to find my review of them in Gibraltar. 11. One of the best songs on the albums. The only thing I've got against it is that the computerdrums at the end are well, a little out of place. The album is very good too. Well worth a try. 12. The first release by SI (the original deleted). New artwork and this song is quite a good one from this very fine release (a lot better than the second part, I must admit). 14. The song is not the best on this album, but read my review of this album somewhere else in this collection.

Conclusion

For the price a nice compilation of the label.
