Summary

In its ongoing quest for, em, well, err, justice or something, the Rick Ray Band brings us another release.

The music

So, what can I tell about this Rick Ray album that hasn't been said about past efforts? The music remains blues rock, with at best the slightest hint of progressiveness. Lots of guitars, most of the album accompanied by Noch's or Ray's smoky, raw vocals, at times augmented by Schultz's brass. Well, okay Noch's vocals are new starting from the previous album, but hard to separate from Ray's. The vocals don't sound as angry as before, but the lyrics keep their Don Quichote feel. This mellowed out sound makes for a somewhat more balanced album than the old ones (as did the couple of albums before this already). The sound is still pretty flat and dull, though. Despite the switch to a band formula instead of solo artist with accompaniment, I find Ray's albums keep their character, to the point of disinterest.

Conclusion

If you liked what went before, you'll like this. If you didn't, you won't like this either. If you wanna give Rick a shot this is a good enough bet. At the end of the day this is decent sounding blues rock, nothing more, nothing less.
