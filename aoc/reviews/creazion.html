Summary

Having at least one album their name, the word reached me that their latest one was a killer. Is it true?

The music

If you are looking for massive playing, quiet, intricate and melodically interludes sung in pleasant Italian. If you are looking for heavy guitars sawing, whining, crying and screaming. If you want intricate rhythms, lots of breaks and tempo changes and this all with a schizophrenia approaching the latest King Crimson, but still very much having their own style, combining heavy powerrock of the nineties with early seventies melodic phrasings, good production, good playing throughout and a pleasant voice besides. If you think that besides KC and VDGG there are too little bands that approach that level of intenstity, that dissonance without becoming meaningless, then look no further. This is where it's at. The only track that doesn't bristle is the more subtle and easygoing bonus track.
