Summary

After having won a festival, this seemingly new band was able to record over 27 minutes of their own work which they released by themselves. I'm not sure where you can get this thing, because they have no distribution yet. Also I don't know anything about how much it would cost, if ordered directly from the band.

The music

The less melodic side of the band reminds me a lot of Primus including the humour and the vocals. The music can also be quite jumpy and quirky as is the case with the Canadian band. Most of the tracks have at least an echo of it (save 6 and 7) and some are prominently in this vein (like 1 and 3).

Where Primus is quite one-sided in their approach this band however has a definite "other face" to it with diversified diversified playing including such instruments as violin, cello and sax. It also seems that in these more melodic tracks they like to have a build-up in their songs getting more intense toward the end of the track, like in track 4 and 7. The latter side of the band is exemplified by 2 and even more on the last tracks of the CD.

The fifth track is rather heavy and contains a very familiar riff (Sollicitere by the Janse Bagge Band and they got it somewhere else as well). The ostentatious presence of a squeaky female voice on this track fortunately has no consequences for the merits of the track as a whole.

The last two tracks seem to form a sort of whole, with the first one being a pianic silence before the storm and the last one builds up to a beautiful emotional crescendo. Very impressive and by far the best on the CD, while the rest isn't bad at all as well.

The artwork is rather inventive with a nice booklet and photos of the bandmembers hardly visible as 'messages in a bottle'. The cover of the album is not too clearly done and if what I see is what I think I see, this might be for the better (well check it out on my WWW page if it's still there).

Conclusion

A lot of Primus related material and also some Cardiacs are involved, but the great melodic parts (most notably the last track) make this CD also worthwhile for people not normally interested in said bands. The only bummer about this CD is the short length.
