Summary

The debut of this German foursome.

The music

Wonderful artwork decorates the debut of this German foursome. Helped productionally by Grobschnitt E.Roc we get nine complex, modern sounding tracks, that will certainly appeal to lovers of Rush and heavy progressive. Instrumentally it is all well done, but the singing I like less. This is not due to the German accent of the singer, but his voice is not that likable. He also sings too much in too little time. For a better stage presentation I advise them to take on a singer. Still this is no reason not to listen to this disc.
