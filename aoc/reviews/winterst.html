Summary

The companion piece to A Midsummernight's Dream.

The music

Well, what can I say about this companion piece to A Midsummernight's Dream, that they released less than a year ago? Nothing much I guess. Red Jasper is still very much the same since that last piece of work (which was the idea it seems), but I think their next work will be different. The music is folky with minstrel's vocals, subtle and melodic with some rough edges to round it off and keep you from sleeping. Worth your while to say the least. If you have the companion piece, you should think first whether you will want this one. A pre is that this a companion piece so if you really like it you might like to make the package complete. On the other hand if you think it's okay, but enjoy it only because it's the first of the kind you have, think again, because this new album is like the first, like songs in a concept album are linked together. If you have neither try on any of the albums (the one most easily acquired I'd say). For me, it's like with Auel books, you like the first one you experienced best.
