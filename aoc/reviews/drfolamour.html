Summary

Got this CD unexpectedly from IO pages for reviewal. Here's the English attempt.

The music

The kernel of this band are both Marcs, responsible for the writing and a lot of the playing. The first track on the album seems to show that we have a band here that likes to play along the lines of Threshold and Queensryche, but as it turns out, this is deceptive, although heavy guitars abound throughout. No, it turns out that this some kind of heavy world music (Gabriel's label) and compares nicely to Whistler Courbois Whistler from the Netherlands, although they are far less varied. It seems that in every track om this album they selected another part of the world to be influenced by: Bulgarian ladies on Escalibor, Indian singers on Guanahani, Basta Blues is indeed somewhat bluesy, a little boy sings on Little Boy (ah, how typical), Fatman is sung in a Middle Eastern way, while suite reminds me of the Discipline incarnation of King Crimson with some desperate singing (a la Belew?) and also of Edhels. The moody Quasimodo reminds me of Lone Bear by Tony Levin and features mostly bass. As you see this albums contains something for everyone and as it turns out, the sound is still rather distinctive and the album sounds well as a whole. Still, the music is largely supported by the electric guitar with some space for keys, violin and flute.

Conclusion

A nice album. Very varied in a way and an interesting trip around the globe. References are Edhels and Whistler Courbois Whistler, although Dr Folamour is a little heavier than the first and more varied than the second. This is a plus.
