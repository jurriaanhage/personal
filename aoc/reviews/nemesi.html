Summary

Read on. The presence of Opera Macabra II and IV indicates there may exist more albums of this band.

The music

Many a progressive album starts off magnificently, and when the song then finally has to start, we are confronted with something a lot less appealing. This danger was also present here since the first 1 and a half minute of this cd are great: starting off with a great melody on the keyboard, halfway the guitar sets in and all this at quite a pace. The drums are great too, loud and rumbling. After this intro we come to a guitaristic part, with rather shrill guitars. The vocal part is something different again: Antonella sings in two voices: mainly in the lower regions, her accent is okay and not disturbing and at times, she sings in an operatic way in the background. She has quite a powerful voice although she doesn't use it to the fullest here. With respect to the lyrics: they tell stories in (mostly) English and this English is at times far from being grammatical (she falled with the sound?). I like Antonella a lot better in the desperate, passionate Italian intermezzo and would advise the band to do everything in Italian. After this we almost get a nice guitar solo, almost since they break it off quite too soon, making this song a typical example of a song containing loads of nice (great) ideas, but lacking in composition. Sad. The song is also not very well mixed, making it sound a little muddy at times. Opening in a rather classical style with keyboards and percussion and wailings from the side of Antonella. The title of the track Opera Macabra (Pt. 2 in case you're interested is well-chosen). It took me some time to figure out that Antonella was actually singing the lyrics btw. Like I said before the drums can be nicely low and rumbling, but actually the overall drum sound is not very good. I'm especially not fond of the in general rather dry drum sound and also the way in which up-tempo parts are played with little groove and variation. For the rest this song is rather climactic with lots of changes of pace and classical stylings combined with various guitar sounds and plenty of keyboardsolo's. Nemesi is quite a heavy track with dark, fuzzy guitars and pianic accompaniment. In a way I'm reminded of the dark sound of Incubus Succubus, although this band tried to do this in every song with less variation. This song like the previous ones contains plenty of variation (too much?), classical keyboards, almost prog metal guitars, playing some nice melodies (and some less nice) and the rather low and really high pitched vocals from the throat of Antonella. The shortest track on the album is the five minute The Second Year After The Rain. After the rather stormy three tracks a welcome breather. Opening with piano and being rather moody and sad in atmosphere, the acoustic guitar, also sad, sets in. This continues more or less until the end of the track where Antonella wails a little. A very nice track and since there is little variation, little could go wrong. Opera Macabra IV is not less macaber than part two. Dark music with some nice atmospheric parts, but also heavy guitars. The up-tempo parts sound a bit trite, and, well, many of the negative remarks I've made apply here as well. The closer Blood And Tears is also the longest (not by far though) track on the album and with The Second Year After The Rain the best. Especially the rather free form, atmospheric middle part is quite nice, and the tension is build quite well there. The guitar playing is very varied: simple acoustics, spaceous plucked acoustics, and a nice the addition and again some nice quiet passages with acoustics and rather experimental guitar work. Continuing then with rather abrasive acoustic guitar, very spaceous sounding but certainly a very nice touch. Tension building is done well here up to this point and at last opens into a long appealing guitar solo (the dry drums are forgiven for now, but they might have left them away). I'm not entirely satisfied with the rather poppy up-tempo ending, but...ah well.

Conclusion

This albums contains rather classical, but also heavy and sinister progressive with operatic vocals (although most of the times, the singing is quite low). After all the negative comments, it would go far to say that this is a particularly bad release or anything. As happens often the band abounds in ideas and they have a great singer, but in my opinion they have to learn to compose their ideas in a meaningful way and some more experience in mixing and production would also be nice. I will not mind if they will reuse some of the ideas they had here. Sorry, to see it go to waste.
