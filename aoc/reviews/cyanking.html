Summary

The music

An acceptable piece of work is For King And Country by Cyan (a.k.a. Robert Reed). On this disc we have eight varied tracks, where mostly the influence of Pthe quiet side of Pendragon is felt. The cd is filled with traditional eighties symphonic rock with the obligato keyboardsolo, melodic guitar solo's and inescapable Tolkien lyrics. The vocals are strongly accented (like with Pendragon) and are not high quality. However, most fans will be use to this. What I did not get used to where the inadvertent breaks in the songs that disturb the balance of the songs. Still a nice attempt.
