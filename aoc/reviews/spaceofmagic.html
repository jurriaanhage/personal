Summary

The music

You wouldn't expect an album of which most compositions are written by the bass player to sound improvisational. The structure of the tracks on this do create that impression, never the less. The basis of bass and drums is used to lay assorted guitaristics across, with the bass sometimes jumping in. Maybe that's not the friendliest of ways to describe what this disc is about, but these do go a tad overboard on multiple occasions. During such episodes there is no significant melody. A typical case of musician's music. Sometimes the guitar style reminds of Allan Holdsworth (form a line over there, if you would), but most of the time the music is a little too freaky or unstructured to keep the association alive. Don't get me wrong, there should be room for some freakish experiment within the genre, but if melodical development is -almost fully- sacrificed for this, a line is overstepped.

Conclusion

I admit it: I don't like musician's music much. Especially if that is all there is. Such is the case with this disc. Great technical skills, poor music. Steer away from this one.
