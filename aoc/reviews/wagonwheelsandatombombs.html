Summary

This is guitar virtuoso Sbar's first album.

The music

Even though the brief sax introduction and trumpet closing might suggest otherwise, this album has become what you might expect of a guitar player: very much a guitar album. Sbar alternates electric and acoustic guitars, but in general tracks are guitar driven with bass and percussion in support. His style sometimes reminds me a bit of Allan Holdsworth, noticeably so in I Woke Up This Morning... Having said that, 16 Shades Of Gray has a section in which the play reminds me of Angus Young, of AC/DC-fame. In general, tracks are high speed and very technical, with exceptions such as Drowning In A Vacuum and Blue Harvest, starting with a jazzy feel and an off key guitar, later moving towards more freaky waters again. Diet Soda not only starts pretty laid back. sort of Frisell, maybe, but also features some xylophone and other more melodious percussion. Having said that, a pretty rough transition takes us into another far more freaky section, taking up most of the track. Pity, really.

Conclusion

I have no doubt in my mind that technically Sbar is a fine guitarist, and the drummer makes himself heard pretty nicely too. At the end of the day, though, such a bunch of guitar driven tracks tends to bore me. The balance tipped the wrong way on the technique versus melody scale on this one.
