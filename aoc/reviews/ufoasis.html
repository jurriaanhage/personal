Summary

I really didn't know that this band still existed. he bio states that they still do (something also thoroughly illustrated by this new album), although I'm not sure they always existed. Starting off in 1977 with their Live Floating Anarchy (sounds familiar :)) their last real album was from 1985 (a live album called Been and Gone).

The music

Take some Hawkwind, some Gong, some Hillage and some Ozric Tentacles, although straighter than any of those, because of the early seventies rock influences of people like Hendrix and Clapton, who were then still under the blues influence (well, not just blues... ;). So the music is rather rocky in style, like Hawkwind but with less spacey vocals and a layer of keyboards and spacey sound effects. A rather surprising comparison I was able to make was with early albums by Monster Magnet.

The music might sound like straight rock at first, but does contain tempo-changes, mood-changes and breaks. The overall impression that you get of this music is that it's alternating between dreamy/spacy and rocky, but both very well integrated.

The drummer is always busy, hacking away, the guitar is soaring (although it can be bluesy in for instance track 3), while the keyboards fill up the voids or accompany the melodies by Hawkwind-beeps and tweezes or as in the ambient parts are the prominent instrument. The vocals are not the main point of focus as always. They aren't bad, they aren't great, just about right I guess. The basswork can be quite on the foreground as well (check out the bouncy 4).

It's also worthwhile to note that particularly the rhythm section can be quite bouncy (or reggae like if you will, also take a look at the nick- name of drummer). This seems to happen rather often in this line of music (look at Hawkwind or the Ozrics).

The lyrics are somewhat political or at least begone with the world's troubles and problems (check out 4), like I think they have always been. Just like their source of inspiration and ol' time buddies from Gong (although Gong is more a humourous sort of bunch keeping their messages a bit veiled). The lyrics are more important than to most other psych bands around at this time.

The melodies are usually good although at times they can hold on to a riff for a bit too long, while some melodies are a bit too easy and so I can't say that this album captivated me from beginning to end.

Conclusion

A worthwhile album for lovers of psychedelic and space rock. Very solid and well-executed. If you're into Ozric, Gong, Hawkwind or Monster Magnet, check them out. I like this one better than for instance Theatre, and also better than All Over the Show (the two albums I have). The album sounds good, professional, while retaining some of the edge that it takes to make music interesting. Still a lot of bands like this one is around, but then again most of them have not been around this long.
