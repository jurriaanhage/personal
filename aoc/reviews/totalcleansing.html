Summary

This duo exists since 1993 and according the bio are well-respected within industrial music. Why review it at all then?

The music

The reasons are twofold: I like it and I think that if you are into the darker side of electronic music, you might also like it. The fact that the music is called "industrial" in the bio means quite little. No reference is made to Nine Inch Nails or Ministry on this album. What it does consist of is the following.

A Call To Arms sets the tone for the album. Accompanied by depressing spoken word, it sounds dark and heavy in a slow way. The keyboards are very prominent and sound crystal clear (thinking back the Aragon's Don't Bring The Rain) and the music can even be considered catchy in the sense of being extremely melodic at times. With Gloria you might be thinking Dead Can Dance. The melodies have a sheen of the Middle East lying over them and with the choirs coming into the music I was reminded of the classical album Black Aria of Glenn Danzig.

After the semi-classical and stately sounding Speak My Voice (with the somber spoken vocals recurring), we come to another somber track: Release The World, but here there is even some real singing. In references I was doubting between Pink Floyd and Oasis here, or even Ayreon. Hmm. The production is very clear and very pleasant too.

Regression is more of the same, while Hail The Mushroom Cloud reminded me a bit of Karda Estra having lots of strings in there. Genocide is more- up-beat and is the first song that might be considered a bit like "house". The music continues to be dark. Dreams Of Desolation closes the album with dark ambient (Autopsia) and the sound of cello's.

Conclusion

A very nice album, very well executed. On the whole lacking a bit in variation, but this is a minor point. The style of the music is what I would call dark ambient and somewhat classically styled, but nonetheless very melodic.
