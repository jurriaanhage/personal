Summary

This is the first part of the rock opera trilogy Genius, conceived by Daniel Liverani, apparently of Khymera fame. Outside him it's strewn with guests, each playing their own role, the most noticeable ones being Lana Lane, Daniel Gildenlow (Pain of Salvation), Steve Walsh and John Wetton.

The music

Genius opens with the highly peppy voice of the narrator, reminding me of that robot from the Red Dwarf TV series. Having gotten through this, we are presented with the strong opener Without Me Today for this prog metal opera which is quite good, really. As you would expect, there are such sections in which storyline prevails over musical wants, and some sections tend to get a little too hardrocky for my tastes. Having said that, there is some rather ample composing, mainly in such tracks as Without Me Today, Paradox, The Glory Of Our Land and Father.

Conclusion

Narrators often play an important, or at least highly visible, role in the plays they act in. This play's narrator's peppy voice introduces a certain unseriousness (check the second sample for this). Despite undoubtedly being unintended, this does spoil the dramatic effect of this opera. However, all 'n' all it does come out a tentative okay.
