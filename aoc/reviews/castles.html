Summary

Dr. Coenobite is actually Coen Vrouwenvelder, someone who according to biography lives very near to me (some 15 kilometers, which you Americans might regard as the same town, but in my country that's otherwise), in Leiden. Some time ago he released his first album, called My Habit. It got some positive reviews and according to the bio is even obtainable in the US and selling some over there as well. The problem with the album according to the SI review was that the playing didn't show technical ability, but overall people were quite satisfied with the approach. The approach of Coen is a bit like Yngwie Malmsteen's, combining classical music with rock to come to a symbiosis that is overtly modern, but the melodies have that classical thang.

The music

Most of the disc is instrumental, though Nynke throws in her voice on a few of the tracks, and I wish she hadn't. She just doesn't add anything, though I must admit that this album would have become quite boring if it were just instrumental. The problem is that the classic stuff with the music and the very accessible melodies make the sound of it kind of cheesy to say the least.

Although his former album was to have been quiter heavy there's now more space for the acoustic parts, but that doesn't help as well, it only makes the songs sound alike, leaving them with no identity at all.

This might sound very negative to you, but I do think that there might be people who enjoy this tuff. For instance people who like the classical influences in a song like Malmsteen's Dreaming. Additionally the style is also Gandalfian, though a number of notches below that.

The album has it's moments for instance the long Garden of Spirits is kind of good, almost New Age I'd say, with piano and acoustic guitar. Not a song to stand on your chair for.

Conclusion

Interesting point is that the intro to the second song is very much like the intro to FAF by For Absent Friends (a neo band), but don't let that make you think this is neo, not after what I said before. Might be worth a try if you accidentally find it in your shop, because not worth an extensive search.
