DeafDialogue - EP

Artist: DeafDialogue
Title: EP
Label: LAP
Length(s): minutes
Year(s) of release: 2004
Month of review: [08/2005]

Line up

Domenico Solazzo - drums
Marja - vocals
Ari - guitar
Manu - bass

Tracks

1) Triton 3.35
2) Megalomania By Association 3.09
3) Contain And Create 4.00
4) Night After Night: The End Of The World 4.23

Summary

An EP by Domenico Solazzo and band. Domenico also releases music under his own name.

The music

Triton has a very live feel. The female vocals are somewhat nasal, and I am fond of them. The music is quite metal like with plenty of heavy rhythm guitars and pounding drums. A bit formless this, and of demo sound and production quality. Megalomania By Association continues the line, even a bit fiercer than before. The vocalist now starts to growl at times, and I think I like her better that way.The rhythm section is like a runaway train.

Contain And Create is a bit more tuneful at first, but soon we are back at the rhythm guitar drone of the previous two tracks. This song does have a hasty, but fiery lead guitar solo, but the drummer simply hacks away. The vocalist does get rather loud at three quarters down the track. She should try to sing more like that. On Night After Night: The End Of The World we get more electronics, and a bit more melody as well. The melody reminds me a bit of Madonna's Like A Prayer but hidden beneath a layer of noise, and sung in a monotonous fashion. The final part contains experiments on keyboards.

Conclusion

This very demo like EP has four tracks that are not likely to be interesting to readers of this website. It is full of plenty heavy material (think Meshuggah, but with singing), that to me sounds rather formless. I am not too happy about the vocalist either, her voice is a bit too nasal and whining.
