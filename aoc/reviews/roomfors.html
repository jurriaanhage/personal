Summary

Having one album to their name, this is my first encounter with this band.

The music

It starts out extremely slow with some weird noises and then an extremely slow melody. You think, what can become of this and to my surprise when the vocals start the song gains depth. The vocals are not beautiful or something, but the song is so intimate, as a soldier tells about his life and how they need more men: "You sometimes win, but always lose". A great track, sluggish, but in a good way. The chorus is majestic with some great keyboard stuff. Strange, that although the song is quite long it doesn't really bore or something. And the only change apparent concerns the weird sounds in the middle.

The next track is more sunny and it has got something (but what?) but on the whole not very impressive.

The next track is again (more even than the first), a very subtle one, very dramatic and and a moving ballad.

The fourth one is heavy again (yep, back and forth we go), with heavy guitars a bubbly bass and a good melody. The song is sung rather strangely, while the keyboards a rather obvious, but the melody is alright.

In the fifth trackwe find yet another good melody. A very sad one. The track is mid-tempo and has a Genesis feeling to it. Again, intimate like the first track but more rockish. At once, the tempo picks up and the song becomes more sunny and optimistic.

On Hunting Old Ladies it's more or less mid-tempo rock with an organ, some heavy underwork, and a nice melody. The guitarsolo and afterwards the piano playing are a bit too romantic for some probably, although the melody is still nice.

Seven has an off beat rhythm and is sung quickly. For the rest it kind of conforms to the rest of the album. A majestic keyboard melody graces this track. Again the singing is very distinct and I admire this a lot.

The Famous Hill is a flowing track with some jazzy influences and comparable to the other tracks.

The closer is rather simple with a female singer instead of the mostly male singing on the rest of the album. Also some piano and overall rather friendly.

Conclusion

Very distinct vocals, good melodies throughout and although the tracks sound accessible, there's some quirkiness in most of them. You might might rank them under poppy progressive with a difference. The balladlike tracks are the most likeable IMO. I certainly like the keyboard sound, which is very warm and melodic. The guitar tends to be more of a rhythm guitar in the heavier tracks, than anything else. A lot of good tracks, which makes me take the more jittery and unbalanced ones for granted. A pleasant surprise, although I wonder how they hold up after a large number of listens.
