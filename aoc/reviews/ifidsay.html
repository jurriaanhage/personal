Summary

See the last album.

The music

This is not an album actually, but a single and a rather long one as well. Unfortunately it doesn't bring that much new stuff to the WoS collector, because only the version of Trees of Stone is new. For a single the level is rather high although it tends to dip in Heaven (and If I'd Say as 2nd worst), but the two other songs originally from Homesick are quite good. Trees of Stone is an old time fave and I've to admit that also in its acoustic version it still is a good song (I like the older versions better, however) and it also contains a lot of rhythm.

If I'd Say is a rather strange almost acoustic with much rhythm and obviously WoS. I'm quite sure this will not be a hit single, but it probably isn't meant as such, but more in the hope that airplay will result in albums getting sold. Still, the problem of getting airplay is a rather stringent one. I'm not an avid radio listener, so I really can't tell whether their approach has met with success, but I very much doubt it (if so I'll probably read about it in SI magazine).

Conclusion

For the people that are interested inwhat WoS sounds like and are too lazy to look at my album reviews, I'd say that they make melodic rock, vaguely on the progressive side (getting vaguer by the album it seems) and they tend to make music in the Rush vein. The melodies are quite good (although Heaven is too sweet for my taste), but for the prog listener not really interesting music. If you are interested, try Homesick. You get the two best songs (3 and 4) of this CD single and more. If you don't like that, buying this single has no use either; if you like it you might then like to complete your collection.

It's rather sad however that this CD single contains more good songs than Face the Truth of which If I'd say and Heaven were culled.
