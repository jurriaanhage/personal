The music

An album inbetween albums. To soften the long wait for Mouse after the strong and well-received The Meeting and Don't Bring The Rain, the band decided to release an album with older, dustgathering material. The music of Aragon is dominated by the usually quiet keyboards and the emotional harsh vocals of Les Dougan. Most striking song on this album is the 20 minute epic Rocking Horse: dark and menacing with some more pacey passages. The other songs are also generally of a dark and quiet nature, excluding the up-tempo closer Changes. The tracks Ghosts can be found here in two versions: studio and live and I admit preferring the former. The Tangerine Dreamish sinisterness of the music is more felt in that case. The music is good, although the level of the other releases is not obtained.
