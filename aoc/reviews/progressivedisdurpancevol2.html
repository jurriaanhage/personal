Summary

The second in the series, somewhat more expensive because of the beautiful artwork. The first one was certainly a nice compilation of, to me, thoroughly unknown German bands. This compilation was made with Europe as a whole in mind and with bands that are sometimes less obscure.

The music

The first track is a familiar one, since I already have the disc and even reviewed it for this webpage. The track chosen is not that great for sampling this band. The song is an instrumental, does show great diversity and will remind people mostly of Saga (especially the beginning) and some more thoughtful passages as well. A very nice track, but it doesn't let you sample the vocals. Mayfair also already recorded, called Die Flucht on WMMS. Not specifically prog this band was a borderline case. On Trip we hear a Hawkindish type band with distorted guitar and full blown spacey sounds, sounding more "progressive" than on aforementioned album. Dionysos is a band that I did not know. This double track opens quietly with twinkling and sparkling piano. The guitar playing contains a lot of feeling, but the singing, the lyrics and drumming sound a bit too straightforward and sound a bit too amateuristic. Third Voice is a band from the U.S. Just two guys making music (well one making it, the other singing). The music of this "band" comes close to Enchant (both in music as well as vocals) with an appealing melody on the keyboards but also some more rowdy guitar parts. From Where You Stand is quite a nice track and indicates where the band stands. [If their new disc has not been reviewed for my homepage yet, then watch this space.] Blank is a, to me unknown band, here featured with Perfect Illusion. The music of this band is rather accessible, but well-crafted. Nice bass work here. The music is very English and I'm reminded of bands like It Bites, Ark and Big Big Train. Not very renewing, but it sounds good. Twin Age is a rather well-known band having two records beneath the belt (and not forgetting too reviews by me on the website). They are a good band and almost any track will do as an introduction. The band is rather Genesis like in atmosphere, but I also feel them to have a sound of their own, especially because of the dreamy vocals. Moonlit plays Followed By A Memory for us, the first progmetal song (strangely enough I would say). A good track. Dramatic, but sensible and not too much riddled with thrills and fills. The guitar solo is also nice. The vocals are a bit down so if you like 'em high, this might not be for you. The title of WW's song, Wanna Know does not promise much. However, the song is quite distinct from what I expected. It is a sort of light form of Zeppelins Kashmir (turning louder later) with quickly sung lines. Strange, in one sense rather AORish, but much too adventurous. Moondaze's Life Will Live On is an up-tempo one with quick rhythm, doubled vocals, with the music going into the direction of somewhat accessible progmetal. Mediocre. Getting to the end of the disc with Winterland's Under The Flood and the recurring Esthetic Pale (a big success on the previous DURP disc). The first of these is a rather dark, heavy track. The vocals are a bit nasal, but they sound okay. The guitar solo is not too interesting. The background vocals sound as if are not timed well. Usually this adds to the chaos. Esthetic Pale has a live track now, See You There. By far the longest track, it takes some time getting underway. Mostly strumming on the acoustic guitar in the beginning. The vocals do not sound as well as on the disc, a bit wavery. A rather disappointing reintroduction.

Conclusion

After the pleasant surprise of the first disc, I am somewhat disappointed with this one. This might be due to the fact that I already know quite many of the bands present, so there is no surprise effect, or maybe because my expectancy was higher to begin with. Most songs are quite nice, and of course Twin Age is a band you should get to know. Also some promising new faces have presented themselves, such as Blank (accessible) and Moonlit (dark).
