Summary

From somewhere in Scandinavia, this band comes. I've guessed they're from Sweden, but I'm not sure. Maybe they don't know themselves.

The music

What a surprise: a synth intro. Happens rather often lately. Not that I mind and in some cases it's the best part of the album. Not this time, because the first few tracks of this album please me a lot. Take Off is a threatening track with a heavy bass, nasal, raw singing and good melodies, also in the chorus. A reference that comes to mind is Dilemma, although the latter is rather more schizophrenic. The melody of Heavenly is also a good one, and gives rise to a good pianic ballad. Introverted Mirrors is more or less the breakpoint on the album. It's a good track, but here already do I have the nagging feeling that the spark that I got from Dilemma will not come here. The singing on this track is varied enough and also the guitarplaying reminds me of KC, being rather repetitive and haunting. Both the fifth and sixth track are not very interesting, mostly because the vocal melody is not very striking. The raw voice of the vocalist is also something that tires easily so beware. It is true however that he has an original way of singing. When we then come to Colours I find that on the one hand I'm reminded very much of things like Another Red Nightmare, but on the other hand the vocal parts of the track are sweet. A combination that does not work well, although of course I like the ARN part. On the Other Side is a nice track with the drums pushing the music on and giving some substance to the music and nice alhugh familiar keyboardstuff. Plingeling is what you might expect: acoustic and plingeling, while the long closer shows that the band still has to mature.

Conclusion

Where Dilemma tends to go "over the top" this band decided to be a little "strange" but not too and I like them less for it. Also the melodic content of the album and the structure/composition of some the longer songs on the album leave something to be desired. Back to the drawing board?
