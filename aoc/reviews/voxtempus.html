Summary

The music

This mini album, probably something of a demo, judging from the somewhat muffled sound, presents a trio of prog metal tracks in an Enchant vein. Dan Reed is mostly known -to me anyway- from the funkrock oriented material he made with his self named Network. I seem to remember his vocals were more secure back then, but that could be me. Anyway, with his high pitched vocals and singing style Reed could still easily step in in Enchant any moment. In general one could state that this band doesn't sound as tight as Enchant does, but then again: they are almost machinelike in their steadiness. What About is the best track, with a pushing guitar line somewhere in the middle, really pushing the track forward. Sadly, this is not used as a theme for the song and thus remains a brief surge of strength.

Conclusion

I'm not quite sure what the idea behind this release is. Why would someone with enough public exposure such as Dan Reed release a record which, with all due respect, is pretty much derivative? I consider the Enchant records to be too much the same already. Another band near-copying them therefore doesn't exactly strike me as an attractive proposition. The end result is pretty decent still, but not very interesting.
