Summary

The first album I have that takes exactly one hour. Seems mister Marianni has been making music since the seventies. Still, this is the first time I've ever heard of him. I do know Patrick Rondat who was responsible for at least two metal albums.

The music

Another concept album. The story plays on an oil rig, in the future the only way to gain energy. The story is rendered summarily in French and English while the lyrics are also given in German and Japanese. This is by the way the first time I ever heard of Luc Marianni although he's been at it since 1979 and this is his sixth album. Another person playing on this record I do am familiar with: Patrick Rondat, who made at least two solo metalalbums. The singing is done by Caroline Crozat, who can do a lot with her voice, but unfortunately her voice is not devoid of accent. On this album, the high and clear voice of Crozat goes hand in hand with the keyboard dominated melodic music of Marianni. The music is on the whole rather mellow and so in the eyes of some might be not be progressive enough. It can happen that tempo changes are found in a song, but they are not very striking, and this indicates good dosing more than it does trying to be accesible. Still, save Rondat's guitarwork, which is able to give some bite to the music, the music on this album is more meant to be beautiful than anything else and also the fact that the album has no ascertainable climax leaves me wanting at the end. On the whole the music can be described as being laid-back. A comparison with Pink Floyd can be made, also rather laid-back I think, and also the vocals do approach that "Great Gig in the Sky" quality, if you know what I mean. Also, comparisons with both Rick Wright's and Mickey Simmonds' solo albums can be easily made. In all cases, the keys dominate and the music is not veru uplifting. However, the Wright's album is a lot darker, while Simmonds is more songoriented and bombastic.

Conclusion

Precisely 60 minutes long this album contains nice melodies and good musicianship, but the more interesting quicker pieces Human Touch 1, Sea Riders and Up and Down can't help the album being a bit tame.
