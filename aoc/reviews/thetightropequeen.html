Finders Keepers - The Tightrope Queen

Artist: Finders Keepers
Title: The Tightrope Queen
Label: ArtPop Records
Length(s): 8 minutes
Year(s) of release: 2001
Month of review: [11/2001]

Line up

Anders Carstensen - words and music (and everything else?)

Tracks

1) The Tightrope Queen 3.55
2) Personality 4.21

Summary

Taken from the album Proud, with which I am not familiar, this is a kind of sampler single.

The music

The first track has good pop with good guitar playing. The sound is that of rather modern pop. The second track Personality sounds less soundwise but is more progressive. Part of the vocals sound a bit like Subterranea (the song), but for the most part I was reminded of Ultravox and maybe The Associates. I do think the recording quality of the second track could be better, but the music is okay. I would prefer also to review the album next time, two songs tell me only little.
