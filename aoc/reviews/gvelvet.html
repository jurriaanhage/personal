Summary

An almost solo album from the Netherlands.

The music

A small symphonic rock production self produced, written and played (besides some bass) by George Konings. Everything okay on this album at first listen. The singing is acceptable, the melodic gutarsolo's around which the songs are composed are quite alright and the compositions themselves are quite reasonable as well. Nothing to complain then? Of course I have. Notwithstanding the nice melodies, the album does not sparkle and on the whole the cd tends to be a bit boring or uneventful (in my old review I noted here "similar to Wish You Were Here", but I've matured since then and I really can't let that remark stay).
