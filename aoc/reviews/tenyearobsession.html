Summary

Some homegrown material from Canada being recordings by this man made in the period 1984-1993.

The music

With his typical voice Symons works his way through the hasty Beware The Future. It has a bit of the feel of punk music. After a sinister intermezzo with a bobbing bass we come to some contrary guitar playing. Quite comparable the music on Pacified by Uneven Steps (of which Symons was one of the guilty parties). The punk feel stays on Killex which is sung in a biting way. Who Cares? is maniacal, but not interesting. This impression stays on What's My Problem? and The Last Air Mask. This is more like alternative rock and it rambles. Ties sounds more psychedelic, but the quality is even less: the drums sound amateuristic. Turn Your Motor Down is a vague sounding cover of a song from Pacified.

Things turn more proggy with the KC neuroticism of Infinity/Uninfinity (read discipline instead of infinity). The album continues with the percussive violence of Delay A Pitch Shift. The music can be compared with Rick Ray here, a bit like underground psyche. Quite drive and experimental.

A Death Song is more in the Zappa style, meandering guitar rock with experimental blues influences, while A Friend I Know is percussive rock 'n' roll. San Francisco Fog is more melodic, a vague track with lots of keyboards, but also sounding a bit abstracted.

Excess Dominates My Life brings us something jazzy, Cool Bass Line means a return to before track 8. What Society is quite nice with lots of guitar, lots of pace. Whale Song is a noisy track while Swear Song For Kids is an aggressive sounding closer.

Conclusion

This album is more like a demo thing, with some tracks having progressive touches (starting with track 8), mostly a type of underground rock, and not really that interesting.
