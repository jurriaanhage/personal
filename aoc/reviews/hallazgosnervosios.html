Summary

The follow up band to the "legendary" Mexican RIO band Nazca, here is another RIO outfit. The classical influences will be large judging by the instrumentation. Interesting artwork as well. Btw: track 11 eleven is not noted on the back of the CD, but it is mentioned inside.

The music

Well, this live album tends out to be quite good and also accessible as RIO goes. The sounds is extremely classical as are mos of the compositions. Alright, the title track starts out as a cacophony and also Estudios can be quite hectic, but most of the stuff is subdued, dark and atmospheric music played on classical instrument, in classical modes.

In some of the tracks a more ethnical sound is apparent (Middle Eastern) and this of course only helps to make the music more varied and interesting.

I'd say that this album is not just interesting for those RIO fans among you, but for just about everyone, fond of classical music and not afraid of a little adventure.

Conclusion

So, to conclude a few moments in which the average listener might inadvertently go for the volumeknob, but most of the time this album is a very pleasant listen and certainly something different from the run of the mill neoprog of today.
