Summary

After their highly acclaimed The Blood Of The Berry, this is the second album in the trilogy.

The music

A Damp Preserve is the opener of the second magnification of their first album. The vocals as on the previous record have a dreamlike quality, soothing, often with harmonies, but always in line. The Fly-Man And The Snake has a great piano melody and gets more involved as well. As on the previous album, the music comes in waves: sometimes we are in a lull, then again the waves go higher, in an up and down movement. Finders Keepers is a vague track. The Moody Bluesy vocals remain, but the music has that relaxedness of Pink Floyd. The music is on the whole self similar (happens often with concept albums as you must know), hence I will point out some of the more extraordinary features. Hush features some spoken vocals, while The Engine Roars is somewhat classically arranged. Tribes sounds a bit tribal with plenty of percussion and Playground Politics has some (soft) percussive piano. Mia's Game is a more acoustic track that goes back to the acoustics after a climactic part. Misha Superhero opens in as a jam, but becomes a flowing rhythmic track with a keyboardsolo no less and afterwards some repetitive piano. The title track is one of the more memorable tracks, because of its great vocal melody. Quite spacey in places this one. Channels is an instrumental, quite powerful with its organ and keyboards, and its driving guitar solo's and washes of drums. The album closes with the soothing and also sad Soil in which the beautiful theme of the album is recaptured.

The lyrics are all seemingly symbolic, but they are good. An extra reason to like this band.

Conclusion

Not worse nor better than their previous one, the music on this album borrows a lot from Pink Floyd in their "relaxed" approach to music. Soaring solo's, well sung harmony vocals, good (but similar) melodies and once in a while an outburst in the music help making this album into what it is: a collection of strongly related thoroughly melodic and enjoyable songs.
