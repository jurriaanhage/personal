Summary

If I had to name one artist who I thought would record and release every idea he had ASAP it would definitely be Rick Ray. Rick managed to release no less than 21 very full length albums in a three year period (this one appears to be so full length that the disc's capacity pre-empts the final track). You can imagine my surprise when I got this collection of out-takes and unfinished pieces from recording sessions in 1999 and 2000.

The music

As you would expect the album is mix of instrumental guitar work and vocal tracks with a strong basis in guitar work. Some songs are strong, some are slow, all are Rick Ray. His vocals blend in with the music pretty well, but I can hardly make out any words. Although none of the songs has near perfect sound quality, the differences in quality make clear that this disc features outtakes from different recording sessions. Can't Take The Plastic is a driving song with a strong guitar riff, and Ricks characteristic vocals. One Dead, One Left is a somewhat up tempo riff driven track. Headed Towards Nothing features some swirling and whirling guitar playing. Beginning To Scream is a short driving staccato track. Love Blinds Hate is another pretty strong and driving song.

Conclusion

No doubt about it: this is a Rick Ray album. With its occasional good song, but most are just okay. And after half an hour you've had your share of Rick for the day. Unfortunately the disc still lasts another 45 minutes. For most it would be best to wait for a Best Of, but I'm not sure that will be coming.
