Summary

Well, being the second artist to send me a CD without telling me first is also quite something. Yep, putting my address on does seem to help. Anyway on a more serious note: this mini CD played, composed etc. by Andrew himself (can I call you Andrew) has been released as a limited edition (well, in this finite universe anything is limited) by EARecords. He hopes, that by support of people buying this CD he will be able to continue his music making. Most on this album is electric, because he can only play the drums.

The music

A short album: a short review. Well, not necessarily of course. After the first listen I had to think a while to determine whether this was progressive enough. I now think it is. Most of the songs are rather poppy to my taste, but especially the instrumentation and the signatures and breaks are not things often encountered. Musically there's a lot of keyboards and rhythm, and in the first track, the first reference I think of is Patrick O'Hearn on Between Two Worlds. What is important on this album, is the mood it evokes, because much of the music is in some way selfsimilar. All tracks evoke a kind of loneliness, a coldness and distance. This clinicalness is also apparent (for me at least) on parts of Danger Money by UK and I do find a melodic ressemblance there (in the last track, at the end).

Anything more to tell about this CD? Yes, for instance that Walk is faster than Run which is rather strange, although people in the city tend to run (jog) slower than they walk.

Conclusion

For the rest it is a pleasant (mini) album to listen to and especially for the late evenings. On the whole it is quite relaxed, has good melodies throughout and is nonobtrusive.
