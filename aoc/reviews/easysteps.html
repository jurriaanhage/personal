The music

Funky jazzrock, that is what the Gabor Nemeth Group brings. No prog, so that is why I am writing short review here. The first track is a good example: the rhythms are funky, the drummer, Gabor Nemeth himself, has a tight and fresh sound. The guitar playing consists of typical seventies rhythm guitar and some more Steve Morse like electric lead. The music is certainly melodious, with occasional lead play by a horn section. The title track has some big band elements and a strong groove. The playing continues to be fresh, never wanting to sound complicated, simply good time jazzrock with some strong lead guitar bits and bass that rumbles throughout. A song like Bullfrog Blues I kind of lose interest. On The Moon is a bit more abstract with a large role with coldish sounding keyboards. The drone of the rhythm section is very train like (chugging). As long as the pace keeps up, the music is alright with me, then I do not notice that much that I tend to need more variation and such. The pace goes high for instance on Speed Contact, but also the light metal of Plastic Boogie. Metal Tango is has some nicely fluent Latin percussion, while After Midnight is a weak follow up, very trite. The album closes with a drum solo.
