Summary

This band from Holland has been at it for some time now, but this is the first of their albums to reach my ear.

The music

The intro is nice. A little science fiction here and at first glance this is some sort of concept album, about a malfunctioning of some sorts giving rise to all kinds of nasty consequences.

Anyway, the music isn't hard to describe, because this is melodic power prog metal. Not too bad either, rather good actually, because the melodies are in rather good and on the whole they remind me quite a lot of the first Threshold CD and also somewhat less of Queensryche. The vocals of Timmer are good, sometimes high, but it's not high all the time and that's a relief. A singer with balls, one says in my country (well in Dutch then). Technically it is well executed, but I expect nothing less from band like this.

Although most of the music is bombastic and the energy level is high, there's some space for a ballad: Realm of Darkness is a typical ballad, but at the end it has some rough edges as well. Emotional track.

Because of the high importance of melody and somewhat less because of the variedness of the music, this is definitely interesting for people who enjoy some of the names I've already coined.

Conclusion

Maybe not very original (there isn't much breathing space in this line of music, because your either a follower of DT or of Queensryche), but very likable. It has to be said though that the music has a sameness to it (but see my remark on the previous line).
