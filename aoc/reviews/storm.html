Summary

Abbfinoosty is probably, originally, Asif + Bob + Tony, but now Asif seems to have taken over and the other two can sometimes play along. He's also responsible for the music and such. I do not have their first release and Cyclops states to have high expectations, this being their most expensive production.

The music

The first that strikes me is the lack of lyrics. Some of the songs on this album are also worth reading a bit, although not TOO seriously. Anyway, the music is rather good, but also straightforward and holds way somewhere between melodic rock and space rock. Especially the latter is very noticable in the beginning of the album, with When the sun explodes a rather typical rocktrack with a nice intro. Interstellar is actually one longdrawn solo, with a spoken intro (and some nice synth work). Also the Hawkwindish synth aounds add to the spacerock feeling.

Cyberspace is a little monotonous with a catchy refrain and hardly audible verses. Then however we get the sad ballad Saddest Girl, which isn't rich in lyrics, but is a very beautiful song, with sensitive guitarplaying. Then we get the folky Love like this, the spooky Churchyard, the lamenting Hell or high water adn the rather sweet Dream, while the closer You'll never make it is almost a lullabye (weren't it for a quite menacing intermezzo). Again, the CD has a ghosttrack, which we might call Recipe for Power.

Conclusion

Not a very good album, and not very progressive either. Still, the album has its merits with some crafty songs and vaired approaches to music, while staying in the melodic rock genre, mostly. Central in the music is the guitar, but the compositions do not suffer from this. The singing is good. So, not a must have, but a pleasant album.
