Lizard interview March 21st 1998

Introduction

This file contains the English transscript of the interview with the Polish band Lizard. We started with the singer, Damian Bydlinski, but later on other members involved themselves with answering our questions (after discussing with each other heavily in Polish). The interview was held in De Opsessie in Bussum during the Dutch Progressive Rock Stage festival held there on the 21st of March 1998. The interviewers are Jurriaan Hage and Roberto Lambooy,

the interview

Without asking a question Lizard started by telling something about what will happen in the future.

We will record a new album, to be released in October 1998. Next year, in the first months, January or February, we would like to have our next CD in the Polish shops. This album is in the Polish language. Now we have five songs, we need about seven, eight songs for the next album. Today we played two new songs, No Mercy pt. 1 and No Mercy pt. 2. Part 1 will open our new album and part 2 will close.

So was that "part 2" song that you played No Mercy part 2 or Faktury Czasu II?

We played 21st C.S.M., No Mercy pt. 1, Selfportrait, In the land of the emerald lizard with bolero, In the garden of destiny and No Mercy pt. 2. It's a short concert.

Okay, so it was No Mercy pt. 2.

The organisator told us to play only a short time.

That's the way it is with festivals. You started out with Schizoid man so that reveals what seems to be an influence. In the intro of the second song I heard something of UK. I'm not sure which song, but...

Our new songs are King Crimson, UK. They are the best groups and they are an inspiration to our group. And eh and maybe you hear UK...

These references are mostly heard in the instrumental parts. In the vocal parts it's more like pop.

Our English bad

Our Polish is bad too. I heard you played with ELP in Poland as support.

It was a big surprise for us. Three days before the concert we were called on the phone. They told us to we would play before ELP. This is good. The concert was very nice, a very exciting thing for us.

What did the audience think of you.

They reacted very good.

Did it also help?

We can't really tell. Some of the people did react to it, but we can't tell how many. But we played for 5000 people there.

In another interview there was talk of your guitarist Mirek, but it's Worek right?

Yes. Mirek is of Collage.

Is your next album to be released by Ars Mundi?

No, because Ars Mundi is bad.

We also got this impression. It was very hard to obtain CDs and review material for the magazines and it is very hard. I heard Abraxas also changed label, so I wondered what you would do.

We don't know.

At this festival there are three Polish bands. If somebody would come here they might get the impression that everybody in Poland plays progressive rock. Is there a lot of contact.

Not really, it is too far for contact. We are from the South of Poland and Abraxas is from the North.

You've never met them until now.

Oh no we did play with Abraxas and Quidam once in Warsaw and another time. So two times.

It's rather sad that there are quite a few good progressive bands, but it's very hard to get to hear them. Now, of course, you've come over here, but still it is quite hard to obtain or hear your music. Maybe you could work on making it possible to have people order your CDs over the internet. Not a big problem here: short distances, lots of people living together.

We don't know how to euh..

Of course we don't know if there are facilities for this.

In Poland our CDs can be obtained everywhere, but abroad it's very difficult.

Since there is a web page for Polish progressive rock this might also be a way to let people obtain your CDs. By e-mail. There are also quite a lot of people and places on the internet that sell and distribute progressive rock, so you could alternatively use them. It might be good to look into that.

Are music can be listened to on Internet on MP3. We haven't heard it ourselves, but it is possible.

Well you might think about it.

Now about this new CD of yours. Is it comparable to W Galerii Czasu?

Well, W Galerii Czasu, In the Gallery of Time, is a concept album. Our next album is not. Maybe seven eight songs, but not a concept album. Musically it is like No Mercy part 1 and 2, more difficult, but it is good.

Darker?

No, more difficult technically and harmony. More rhythmically complex. We have a new ballad of three or four minutes, very beautiful. A bit like Lucky Man of ELP.

In the direction of jazz rock, UK. For me King Crimson is dark and UK is more energetic. What side did you choose?

Our music is energetic like UK and dark like King Crimson. And we will try to have a Polish version and an English version of our new album.

With English lyrics. It will make a difference I think. If you hear lyrics you understand it is easier to remember them. English also seems easier to sing in, it is more melodic. It would also be nice if you just include an English version of the lyrics with the album, like Abraxas did.

It's a good idea. We promise that with the new album we make a version with lyrics in Polish and English. for the reedition of Gallery of Time we may make two version, English and Polish. This is possible since we have an instrumental version and we only need to record the new vocals.

For me it doens't matter that much what language you sing in, but for general acceptance it might be better to sing in English. Do you have anything to say to our readers: we would like to say hello to all the readers of your magazine.
