Asgard interview 25th of January 1997

Introduction

This interview was done by Jurriaan Hage and Leo Hoekstra in Zwolle at the Day of Dreams in Hedon on the 25th of January 1997. One of the bands present was Asgard and we had a little chat with their keyboard player Alberto Ambrosi (and some interjections by other bandmembers).

The interview

To start off, I'd like to ask you why it is taking so long for your new album Drachenblut to be released, while you were notoriously quick with your first four CD?

The band moved from Italy to Germany (goes on in German and apologizes everyone laughs).

You could also speak German, we don't mind.

I MUST speak English. ....that's the reason why there's a delay in the release of Drachenblut. Now the band is already playing live and our activity is again high. Drachenblut will be released in the Summer of '97.

The move to Germany, was that involved with the fact that you are on a German label Music is Intelligence (WMMS)?

The first three months we worked for this label and then we changed our job and well that's it. But at the beginning we had a job at WMMS.

You mean a real job, nothing to do with Asgard.

We worked for the label, doing things like merchandising and all that.

I was just talking to someone and he informed me that you have left this label. Is that true?

Yes. The next album will be released by another label. We are also thinking about selfproducing. At the moment we do not know exactly.

How far is Drachenblut now? Is everything written, recorded.

At the moment it is not just a question of recording. Everything is written, but we have to practice a lot. We must also arrange or rearrange much of the music.

What about the songs you just played (they played two tracks, one was the titletrack and one other track).

The songs do exemplify the new album. Of course we wanted to play more of the album but we didn't have time, that's a pity. At the moment we can play five songs from the album live. In the next month we will prepare ourselves thorougly for playing the other songs.

The band has quite a number of musicians from Germany, especially for an Italian band. Could you tell us something about the line-up and who plays what?

Our current band consists of just two people from the 1987 line-up, which are Chris (Bianchi D'Espinosa, on bass) and I . The other members are Andrea Stefani, the guitarist who has been with us four two years (playing his first gig in Almelo where the interviewers were both present), and Michael, the drummer, and Dietrich, the singer, who have joined us only recently. We knew them already in Germany for half a year and we found that they are great musicians, but we also found them also good to get along with. They fit very well in Asgard, better than former members.

I feel a big difference in sound and approach between you and other Italian bands. How come?

I think that the main difference is the mentality. Our mentality made possible that the band lived in Germany and can perform Anglosaxon music. Bands in Italy can sometimes be very goud, but they also want to sound Italian. Progressive, but also Italian. I don't mean that this is wrong, but it is the main difference.

You once mentioned Wagner as one of your influences. It struck me then as a concidence that were with a German label and also moved to Germany.

Wagner we like for musical reasons. We made an instinctive choice for a German label as we make music that is more nordic than what it made in Italy. I wouldn't be able to explain that better.

Is the chapter of Goetterdaemmerung and Esoteric Poem closed or is Drachenblut in the same line as these albums?

I think Drachenblut is a cross between the symphonic Asgard from Arkana, Goetterdaemmerung and Esoteric Poem and on the other hand the more rocky and powerful Imago Mundi. I wouldn't go as far as to say that Drachenblut is a mixture of all our former albums, but it contains elements of all of them.

Is a change of sound driven by what the band wants or are the fans also involved?

We decide to make our music, but when we decide which tracks to include on our next album, we also listen to some fans, when they say that the liked that or that track very much during a concert. Sometimes we include a track, because someone told us he liked the song very much.

I looked at some of the equipment and it said Iron Maiden!!!

Ah, was that on the bass amplifier? Yes, that's the amplifier of Chris. We are very ashamed because of this and thank you for pointing it out (to be honest somebody else pointed it out to us). We will take some measures to avoid further problems with that. We hear also heavy metal and actually every other kind of music.

How new are your members? Did you gig with them often?

The singer is a very young guy and he played with a very young band. He is a very good singer, very talented, which is of course why we want to continue with him, but indeed he hasn't played a lot of concerts yet, that's the reason he is a little bit, shy or nervous. But everyone has been like that and so I think he will come around.

In Almelo (two years earlier) the singer (Franco) jumped around a lot and gave an energetic show.

Well, the stage was also very small and also, this was right after Imago Mundi, so the music we played was more powerful. The singer we had then was himself very much into hardrock, so that helps.

Was that also one of the reasons for the sound change between Arkana and Imago Mundi?

There were several reasons for our change. First of all we heard many heavy metal albums at the time and we liked them very much. This was the main reason actually, but we also wanted to show that we can play other music then just plain progressive rock.

What are your plans for the future?

We want to release Drachenblut. What will happen then is not yet known. What will happen is that we will organize another Gothic Gathering Festival in Tremonti in Northeren Italy. Arena will come if they still want to and if they don't ask too much money. That's about it.

Later...

Damn, I did just forget to aks them WHY they left WMMS. The keyboardplayer is now gone, but I'll ask one of the others and it is the guitarplayer Andrea who answers.

Why did you leave WMMS?

It was better for the band to be able to move into new directions, to broaden our horizon and get new input. WMMS is a good label, but we thought it better to look in other places.

Even later...

A member of the German band Chrass (formerly known as Fallen Foe) stated later that a label for Asgard by Asgard and also featuring the members of Chrass might be realized. The idea is their, but it has to be worked out. Also, it was stated that this label will in all probability not only include progressive rock, but other music as well.
