Asia

The first album of Asia was a mega millionseller and including such progressive stalwarts as John Wetton and Steve Howe. These have moved on but Geoffrey Downes and consorts are still making records. Selling quite a few less, but artistically certainly not less interesting. In my opinion, Arena is their best album to date.
