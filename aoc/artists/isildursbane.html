Isildurs Bane

A shame we reviewed so little of these guys, but believe me, they have gotten better and better during their long years. And they are always interesting. Rather a genre to itself, Isildurs Bane needs to be heard by everyone into progressive rock (and other alternative branches of music). I personally like Mind Vol. 4 Pass most.
