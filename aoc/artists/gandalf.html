Gandalf

Gandalf is usually linked with New Age and the like, but that surely sells him short (although it probably is good sales). He has done orchestral, vocal, electronic and progressive works. My favourites are More Than Just A Seagull and From Source To Sea. Very different. However, the cd reviewed here happens to be the worst thing he has done that I can remember.
