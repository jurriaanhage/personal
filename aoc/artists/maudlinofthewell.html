Maudlin Of The Well

Astral Metal as they call it themselves (or is it only the label?). Anyway, an original new band from the States including both blistering metal as well as more acoustic tunes. Bath and Leaving Your Body Map are actually two of a pair, but released as two separate discs. It is a good and easy way to warrant your own page on this website.
