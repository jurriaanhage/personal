Shadowland

One the earlier bands in which Karl Groom and Clive Nolan featured. The band has the tendency to rear up its head once in a while, but nothing ever comes of it. Their first two albums were out on SI Music, but were rereleased on Verglas, the label of Clive Nolan.

During an interview with Karl Groom it came out that he could not even remember the name of the third album of this band, Mad As A Hatter.
