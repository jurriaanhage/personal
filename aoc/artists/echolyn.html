Echolyn

One of the single new progressive bands to make it big, but not making it after the transfer to Sony Music.

After the split the members were spread over bands such as Finneus Gauge, Still, Always Almost and Dark Aether Project. Later they came together again to record Cowboy Poems Free.

It seems their older, hard to obtain records ought to be available again in the future.
