Mostly Autumn

A large band from the United Kingdom with a male and female vocalist. Their music is in the style of Iona, with songs ranging from folky jigs to rather commercial sounding, but well composed, vocal tracks. Later albums show more maturity and a smaller band. The band is gaining a good name and a broad audience starting with their third album, playing support to some well-known names.
