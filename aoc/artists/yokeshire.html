Yoke Shire

An original band from the United States that has been making some headway in gaining an international audience. A Foreshadowing is the single that leads up to their studio album Masque Of Shadows. For such a beginning band they show remarkable maturity. Their rather accessible style is in the direction of Jethro Tull.
