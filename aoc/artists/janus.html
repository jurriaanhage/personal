Janus

A long time ago this band recorded the now very expensive Gravedigger. After years of silence they realized the collector status of the album (mostly in Germany) and decided to starting playing again. After a rock album called Out Of Time (including the fabulous epic Agnus Dei), they've recorded diverse kinds of albums. Some of these are similar to what Enigma and such bands do. Other stuff is more in the line of Gravedigger. Most important man in the band is guitarist and composer Colin Orr.
