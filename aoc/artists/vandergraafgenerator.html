Van Der Graaf Generator

One of the major players in progressive in the seventies. They are not so well-known because they were hard to copy, so only few bands acknowledge them. Although I have no chance of being nostalgic (I have never seen them) I hope I still might. Admittedly, the chances are small, although they all are still alive at this time.
