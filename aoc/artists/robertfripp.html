Robert Fripp

One of the leading guitarist since the end of the sixties. He is the main person in King Crimson. In addition he made some terrific solo albums, collaborated with David Sylvian, Peter Gabriel and Brian Eno to name but a few. His Frippertronics and Soundscapes have left their mark on artists everywhere. Do not leave without reading the long interview. My questions weren't nearly done, but still a lot of talk in there.
