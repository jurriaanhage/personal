Geoff Mann

Geoff Mann was the singer of Twelfth Night and also a priest. During his clergymanship he continued to record music and gig either solo or with his band Eh! (or A) Geoff Mann Band. He was also involved with a band called The Bond. He also recorded a number of solo albums and albums with Marc Catley. Much of his solo material is religiously inspired.

Geoff Mann died of cancer on February 5th in 1993.
