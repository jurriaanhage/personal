Saga

A band of which my opinion seems to differ everytime. They were responsible for the now classic Generation 13 in the nineties, but their history goes much further back to the end of the seventies, when they recorded some pretty good songs and some classic tracks such as Don't Be Late. They have a concise guitar dominated style, which one can find back later in a many a SI Music band. Like Rush (also a proponent of concise progressive rock) the band hails from Canada.
