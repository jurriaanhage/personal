Ywis

A Saga influenced band from Holland. After their debut from 1983 they disbanded to return with a new album in 1995 after SI Music released their self titled debut in 1993. Although the lyrics are a bit clumsy, musically this accessible band has something to offer in the guitarwork of Rinus Hollenberg.
