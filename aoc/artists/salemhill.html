Salem Hill

Good band from the States on Cyclops. Before they came to Cyclops they already recorded three albums. One of these is Catatonia which was rereleased. The other two are available as Cyclops Club releases. Between Catatonia and Not Everybody's Gold they made the highly acclaimed The Robbery Of Murder.
