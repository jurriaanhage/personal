Platypus

Ty Tabor of King's X, Derek Sherinian (left the band after two records) of Dream Theater and later Planet X, John Myung of Dream Theater and Rod Morgenstein (of Dixie Dregs and the Rudess/Morgenstein Project) make up this project like band, that seems to have some staying power.
