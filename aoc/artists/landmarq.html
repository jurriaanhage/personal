Landmarq

A rather accessible melodic band from England in which Damian Wilson sung on the first three albums. Now Tracy Hitchings is at the microphone and the music certainly has not suffered for it. The musicians are all "old hands" in progressive rock. A number of them, for instance, used to play in Quasar.
