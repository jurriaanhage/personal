Discipline

One the most promising new bands. Their Unfolded Like Staircase is certain to be a classic of the nineties. It was only out on their own label, but later it also come out on Periferic. Their music owes quite a bit to Van Der Graaf Generator, but is less harsh, more melodic and also has quite a bit of Genesis influences.
