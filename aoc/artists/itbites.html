It Bites

One of the bright hopes for the eighties, It Bites incorporated catchy tunes and a sense of humour into their songs, speaking to a larger audience than was usual in those days. Dunnery went solo, the others did not hear of at all. The live material here is a recent thing, but does not mean the band is getting together.
