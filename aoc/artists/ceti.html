Ceti

Ceti is a Polish hard rock band with some symphonic leanings as shown on their debut (we are talking the eighties here) Czarna Roza, comparable to Hungarian rockers Omega. Later they became less melodic and less symphonic, while even later they became a typical progmetal band.
