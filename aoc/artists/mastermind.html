Mastermind

A band of which I have actually reviewed all their albums (okay, okay, not the Cyclops Club release). Their albums are of diverse style, but always so that the music is recognizably that band. Quite an accomplishment. After their fully instrumental Excelsior, still the best album in my book, they went for more commercial Lana Lane like stuff in Angels Of The Apocalypse.
